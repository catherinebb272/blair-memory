## Answer to the cron question

**Draft activity almost certainly contributed, but it's hard to isolate.** Multiple compounding factors:

1. **Drafts alone ≠ instant sales.** Etsy's algorithm favors fresh listing *publishes*, not drafts. The 5-sale burst on 6/19+6/21 happened *before* the 6/22 catch-up push, so those sales can't be caused by the 22 drafts uploaded on 6/22. They could have been caused by the prior 14 drafts finally going live, or by the store's general "activity signal" climbing as Catherine was in the dashboard uploading.

2. **This week's 9 confirmed orders (Mon-Fri) is meaningfully above baseline.** Pre-burst baseline averaged ~8 orders / 2 weeks. 9 orders in 4 days (Mon-Fri) is roughly 3× the daily run rate — though June is also a seasonally strong month for sobriety milestones (Jan resolution wave hits 6 months in late June — that explains the cart spike).

3. **Drew published 3 of the 22 drafts on 6/23.** That keeps the "fresh listing" signal active for the algorithm throughout the week.

4. **The 6/24 cart spike (13 items across 6-mo, 18-mo, 2-yr)** is mostly *seasonal* — late June is when January resolution-coin buyers hit 6 months. Catherine noted the spike "went away" — it didn't convert to orders, but the *traffic* was real.

## Honest framing

- **Coincidence vs. draft activity:** The 5-sale burst is *probably* a mix of (a) seasonal June resolution wave, (b) Etsy's algorithm responding to increased dashboard/list-builder activity over the prior week, and (c) some luck. Drafts themselves don't sell — publishes do, and Drew's publishing the 3 on 6/23 was the actual signal.
- **This week:** Confirmed 9 orders Mon-Fri is solid, but I don't have Sat-Sun numbers yet.
- **What would prove the "drafts bump the store" hypothesis:** Track weekly order count for the next 3 weeks (the planned 17-listing rollout window: 12 CSF + CST Spoons + 5-7 CCF). If orders stay elevated or climb through that window, the hypothesis is supported. If they revert to baseline regardless of listing activity, it was just seasonal + noise.

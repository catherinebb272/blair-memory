## Session 2: Journal project pivot

Catherine rethought the journal positioning. Major changes from yesterday's brief:

### Why the pivot
Yesterday's brief was milestone-coin-anchored. Catherine realized: most people don't hit sobriety milestones on time. A journal that says "6 months" and you're at month 3 shames the user. **Milestone-tied journaling is out.**

### New positioning
- **Audience:** Primary = mental health / anxiety / emotional sobriety. Secondary = recovery from addiction. Both served under one positioning line: **"a journal for people on a journey of emotional and mental health, or recovery from addiction."**
- **Pace:** Self-paced. No built-in calendar. User writes the date in. Catherine keeps a journal this way herself (~10 years, sometimes a few times a year, sometimes more). The journal meets them where they are.
- **SKU count:** ONE journal (toss the unisex + alt-female split). More artistic, more cheerful.

### New palette (full + unsaturated)
Brand was rebuilt around **optimistic recovery, not somber plodding through recovery.** Barbara and Drew chose these.

**Full saturation:**
- `#003b88` Ocean Indigo
- `#37c86f` Leaf Green
- `#4d7bcc` Medium Blue
- `#ff5d4c` Sunrise Coral
- `#ffd437` Golden Sunrise
- `#f5efe8` Soft Linen (paper/background)

**Unsaturated (tonal lighter versions):**
- `#4fb6ff` Light Blue
- `#83e2a4` Spring Green
- `#ffeb8e` Pastel Sunrise
- `#f4f8f8` Misty White
- `#ff9b8f` Pastel Coral

Print adaptation OK. Hexes are loose references, not exact requirements.

### Iconography, not the coins themselves
The coin imagery stays **as a design language**, not a featured object:
- Torii gate → new journeys (front matter)
- Fish → mutual support
- Phoenix → rising again
- Waterfall → serenity
- Kraken/lighthouse → lurking perils
- Etc.

Pages are mostly imagery, **subtly following coin icons** but not overtly. Each has a small reflection prompt tied to the image topic. "Reflect a minute on this imagery" feel.

Coins themselves: not in the book. Maybe a single endpiece to consider. (Hold that thought.)

### Page count target
- ~90 daily-style pages (covers 90 days if user journals daily, but not required)
- Front matter
- Some regular recaps
- → 100–120 pages total (decent journal length, fits Lulu spec)

### What stays locked from yesterday
- POD: Lulu, 6×9 coil, 60lb uncoated, matte cover
- Three-SKU strategy: Lulu print ($19.99) + fillable PDF ($7.99) + print-ready letter PDF ($9.99)
- Two-PDF split rationale (iPad fillable vs print)

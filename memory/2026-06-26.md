### Vision gap — IMPORTANT

Catherine's main reason for wanting Composio back was image understanding (the swap-to Gemini Flash that was removed). **Composio's `GEMINI_GENERATE_CONTENT` is text-only** — schema has `model, prompt, system_instruction, temperature, top_p, top_k, max_output_tokens, stop_sequences, safety_settings, voice_name`. No `image`, `file`, `contents`, or multimodal field. Composio wraps Gemini's text endpoint, not the multimodal one.

So the conversational image-understanding gap **stays open** via this path. OCR/document extraction is covered (Mistral, Google Vision, Aryn, JigsawStack, Astica), but "look at this screenshot and reason about it" is not.

## Superlawyer avatar prompt for Gemini

Catherine's brother just got named "Super Lawyer" in employment law (Texas). Her husband joked "should it come with a cape?" — brother replied "should it be pinstripe?" — and Catherine was tempted to make an AI image.

Wrote two Gemini prompts for her:
- **Option A — Pinstripe cape** (the brother's joke): bespoke charcoal suit + tailored pinstripe cape, courthouse steps, "Super Lawyer" shield emblem, vector-meets-watercolor style, white background.
- **Option B — Red foulard power cape**: navy suit + flowing red silk foulard cape, briefcase in hand, single spotlight, painterly digital style.

Also wrote a reference-photo line ("Maintain the likeness, facial structure, hair, and approximate build of the person in the reference photo. Use the photo only for identity — not for pose, lighting, or background.") that she can paste into either prompt if she uploads a photo of her brother.

## What actually happened

Catherine ran the prompt — the result came out exactly as pictured, but **the cape was tricky**: Gemini struggled to make tie-fabric patterns (foulard) read as a cape. The pinstripe/foulard crossover is genuinely hard for image models because they're trained on different fabric archetypes (suit fabric vs. flowing silk) and don't always blend them cleanly.

## Lessons
- Tie-fabric → cape translation is a known weak spot in image generation. If we ever need this kind of "suit fabric as superhero garment" effect again, lead with the cape material explicitly in the prompt (e.g., "silk cape with foulard pattern overlay") rather than asking the model to figure out the hybrid.
- Catherine responds well to prompt *options* with a clear recommended path, plus a short add-on line for reference photos. Two flavors + one extension worked here — that's a good template for future "creative prompt" asks.

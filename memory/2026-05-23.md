## Research: Print on Demand Design Tools

### Kittl
- Free ($0), Pro ($12/mo), Expert ($26/mo)
- Strong AI generation (DALL-E, Flux, Ideogram)
- Built for merch (t-shirts, stickers, mockups)
- Commercial license on paid plans
- Best for POD-focused users

### Playground AI (playground.com)
- Free ($0), Pro ($12/mo), Pro Plus ($36/mo)
- AI image generation (GPT Image 2, Nano Banana, Seedream)
- Templates for social/digital, not POD-specific
- Commercial license on Pro+ only
- Good for AI image generation, then design elsewhere

### Adobe Express
- Pricing: ~$9.99/mo (often bundled with Creative Cloud)
- Firefly AI, huge template library
- Best versatility, integration with Adobe suite
- Free tier very limited

## Research: Etsy Keyword & Ranking Tools

### eRank
- Free, Basic, Pro, Expert
- Keyword research, competitor tracking
- Shop audits, listing optimization
- 2M+ sellers trust it

### RankHero
- Free ($0), Side Hustle ($7.99/mo), Business ($12.99/mo), Enterprise ($49.99/mo)
- Keyword research, niche finder, shop analyzer
- AI tools for titles, tags, descriptions
- Video generator for listings

### Alura
- Free + paid tiers
- All-in-one: keyword research, product research, shop operations
- Marketing & automation (email, Pinterest)
- 800K+ users

### EverBee
- Hobby (Free), Growth ($19.99/mo), Business ($69/mo)
- Keyword Score (built-in ranking metric)
- Product analytics with conversion data
- Trend tracking up to 24 months

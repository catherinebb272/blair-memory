### Google Trends
- Compared: "sobriety card", "sober gift", "mental health card", "addiction card"
- "mental health card" dominates globally (high volume)
- "sobriety card" is lowest
- Feb 2026 had major spike for "mental health card"

### Erank Keyword Data
- "sobriety gift": 2,560 searches/month (Etsy's top term)
- "sober gift": 1,260 searches/month
- "mental health gift": <20 searches (nearly invisible on Etsy!)
- **Key insight:** Google and Etsy are opposite — Etsy buyers search "sobriety", not "mental health"

### Erank Top Listings
- Viewed "Mental Health Card" and broader "Mental Health" listings
- Modest revenue: $204-$869 over 500+ days (long-tail, low volume)
- Humor/sarcastic cards perform better than serious ones
- PaperPawsCo is a major player in this space

### Strategic Insight
- Google = discovery ("mental health card" high volume, informational)
- Etsy = purchase intent ("sobriety gift")
- Opportunity: SEO/alt text captures Google traffic, convert on Etsy with "sobriety" keywords

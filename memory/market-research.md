# ANA Market Research - Keyword & Search Insights

## Google Trends (Worldwide, 12 months)

| Term | Interest Level | Key Insight |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| mental health card | Highest | Dominant search term; major spike Feb 2026 |
| addiction card | High | Second most searched |
| sober gift | Medium | Steady interest |
| sobriety card | Lowest | Minimal search volume |

**Key Learnings:**
- Market frames primarily as mental health, not sobriety-specific
- "Mental health card" has ~2-3x the volume of "sobriety card"
- Feb 2026 spike indicates demand surges are possible (holiday/viral related)

## Erank (Etsy-specific keyword data)

| Keyword | Avg Monthly Searches |
|---------|---------------------|
| mental health gift | <20 |
| one year sober gift | <20 |
| recovery gift | <20 |
| sober gift | 1,260 |
| sobriety gift | 2,560 |

**Key Learnings:**
- "Sobriety gift" dominates on Etsy (2,560) — double "sober gift"
- Mental health framing is nearly invisible in Etsy search (<20)
- OPPOSITE of Google Trends — confirms Etsy users search differently
- ANA's branding ("sobriety milestone coins") aligns with actual Etsy behavior

### Strategic Insight: The SEO Funnel
- **Google** (discovery): "mental health card" = high volume, informational
- **Etsy** (purchase): "sobriety gift" = actual buyer intent
- **Opportunity**: Capture Google traffic with SEO/alt text, convert on Etsy with "sobriety" keywords
- Products essentially live in same space (addiction/sobriety/mental health overlap)

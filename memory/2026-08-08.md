**Posterize Method for Mockups**
- Create initial design in a high‑resolution format.
- Apply a posterize filter (reduce color palette) to simplify colors and highlight key elements.
- Export the posterized image and upload it to Printify’s mockup generator.
- Use Printify’s preview to assess how the design adapts to the product’s printable area.
- Iterate: tweak colors or contrast in the original file, re‑apply posterize, and re‑upload until the mockup looks clean on the product.
- Once satisfied, order a physical sample to confirm print quality.

*Note:* I ended up relying on Printify’s automatic adaptation, which produced a good mockup; ordered a sample for final verification.

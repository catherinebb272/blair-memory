### 4th pass — POD + digital strategy locked

Catherine walked through the production question with real Lulu pricing. Decisions:

**POD vendor: Lulu (locked)**
- Specs: 6×9 coil-bound, 120 pages, **60lb uncoated interior**, **matte cover**
- Lulu's quote: $11.66 print cost
- Retail under $20 on Lulu-direct, ~$24.99 on Etsy
- **Lulu is in Morrisville, NC** — same state as Catherine, 3-5 day US shipping, no customs
- Shipping built into Lulu's price (no separate margin math)
- **60lb uncoated vs 80lb coated:** uncoated is correct for journaling. Coated smears gel/fountain pen ink. Uncoated accepts all pen types.
- Matte cover over glossy: no fingerprints, book-like, matches brand
- Coil over perfect bound: lays flat, survives repeated opens, signals "meant to be written in"

**Strategy: Option C — Hybrid digital + printed (locked)**
Three SKUs per print product:
1. **Digital Fillable PDF — $7.99** — iPad/GoodNotes/Notability, fillable form fields, hyperlinked TOC, screen-optimized
2. **Print-Ready Letter PDF — $9.99** — 8.5×11, no fillables, home-printer friendly, 3-hole-punch instructions
3. **Lulu Print — $19.99** — physical coil-bound, manual Etsy forwarding (~2 min/order, fine at <30/mo)

**Key insight: two-PDF split (iPad vs print)** — fillable form fields fight printable layout. Split them. The "buy both" customer is real, and the differentiated pricing means higher per-customer revenue ($7.99 + $9.99 = $17.98 perceived as two products, not one tier up).

**Why 8.5×11 letter for the printable** — every home printer does it free. Don't mention 6×9 or FedEx cutting. That customer is the Lulu buyer, not the PDF buyer. Self-selecting.

**PDF design rule** — Don't make the PDFs look like the Lulu version. Different fonts/layouts. PDF buyer and Lulu buyer feel they made a choice, not got a degraded version.

**Differentiator noted** — most Etsy sobriety journals don't bother with iPad fillable. Few competitors have done that work. Real competitive moat.

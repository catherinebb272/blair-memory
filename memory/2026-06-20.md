### b5370cf — "Document nano banana quirks + framing preservation fixes from CTV work" (19:41 UTC, +52 lines)

New section at the bottom of the doc: **"Nano Banana (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image) — Known Quirks & Fixes"**. Confirms nano banana as the card-mockup tool of choice over GPT (better output quality, higher credit cost acceptable).

**Issue: AI enlarges artwork relative to card front** — nano banana is decent at fixing drift and elements but weak at re-scaling framing in-place. Documented fixes in escalation order:
1. Drop-in scale-preservation block after the artwork reference
2. Explicit margin call ("10% on all four sides")
3. Numerical anchor ("artwork occupies ~X% of the card front")
4. Third reference trick — flat dead-on photo of just the card front as the cleanest framing target (strongest fix)

**Issue: Table distressing drifts between renders** — explicit stability instruction for wood grain, re-roll if drift persists.

**Issue: Card interior looks mottled when "barely open"** — explicit "interior must appear smooth and uniformly off-white" instruction.

**Root cause if all fail:** nano banana treats white space as "missing content" and crops inward. Source must be the high-res print file (1500×2100+ at 300dpi), not a smaller export.

### Prompt iteration discipline section

New lessons from CTV work:
- Lock the studio first (surface, lighting, angle, focal length, background). Spend credits here.
- Lock framing preservation before doing the full set. 3 test cards across the line.
- Drift fixes (table grain, mottled interior) need explicit per-element instructions.
- Background consistency on nano banana takes prompt refinement.

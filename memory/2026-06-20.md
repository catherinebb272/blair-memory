### b5370cf — "Document nano banana quirks + framing preservation fixes from CTV work" (19:41 UTC, +52 lines)

New section at the bottom of the doc: **"Nano Banana (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image) — Known Quirks & Fixes"**. Confirms nano banana as the card-mockup tool of choice over GPT (better output quality, higher credit cost acceptable).

**Issue: AI enlarges artwork relative to card front** — fixes in escalation order:
1. Drop-in scale-preservation block after the artwork reference
2. Explicit margin call ("10% on all four sides")
3. Numerical anchor ("artwork occupies ~X% of the card front")
4. Third reference trick — flat dead-on photo of just the card front (strongest fix)

**Issue: Table distressing drifts between renders** — explicit stability instruction for wood grain, re-roll if drift persists.

**Issue: Card interior looks mottled when "barely open"** — explicit "interior must appear smooth and uniformly off-white" instruction.

**Root cause if all fail:** nano banana treats white space as "missing content" and crops inward. Source must be the high-res print file (1500×2100+ at 300dpi), not a smaller export.

## Track 2: Saltwash upscale experiments (per Catherine, 2026-06-23 20:55 UTC)

**Context: Saltwash print masters pipeline.** The two largest JPGs in the lineup (16×24 = 8.7 MB, 18×24 = 9.8 MB) are the bottleneck — they push a single Etsy zip over the 20 MB cap, so they're delivered as standalone files (no zipping). Image quality at those sizes is the make-or-break detail.

### Tools experimented with

**For upscaling (Saltwash):**
- **Lanczos** — classic resampling. Cheap, deterministic, fast.
- **Real-ESRGAN** — neural upscaler. Better quality on photographic content, slower, can introduce artifacts on watercolor texture.

**For image generation (Adobe Creative Cloud / Firefly):**
- 5 native Firefly image generators — characterized as "mediocre"
- Several Gemini image generators — **Nano-Banana (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image)** rated most satisfactory
- Several video generators (separate from image)

**For video generation:**
- **Veo** — uses 400 credits per generation
- **Kling** — uses 120 credits per generation (~3.3× cheaper)
- **Decision:** Use Kling (cost ratio makes Veo uneconomical for iteration)

### Settled workflow

**Settled on Lanczos for Saltwash** for the 1k → 2k upscale path:
- Reason: "economically for Etsy" — 2k is enough resolution for Etsy's digital download rendering; Lanczos is free and fast
- For Saltwash print masters, Blair handled the cropping + upscaling for the two largest sizes (per Catherine, the work might be in earlier memory logs)

### Open questions at end of 6/20

- Whether Real-ESRGAN should be reserved for cases where Lanczos shows visible quality loss (watercolor texture risk)
- Whether to ever bring video generation into Saltwash listings (Etsy supports listing video)

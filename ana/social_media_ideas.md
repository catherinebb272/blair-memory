# Sober Life Expectations vs. Reality

## Idea 1
**Expectation:** Wake up early, eat clean, do yoga, change the world.
**Reality:** Wake up late, eat snacks, watch trash TV, don't drink.
**Caption:** "I'll take the win."

## Idea 2
**Expectation:** Be a productive worker bee, finishing all tasks with expert efficiency.
**Reality:** Struggle to focus, spend hours on one task, and celebrate small victories.
**Caption:** "I'll take the win."

## Idea 3
**Expectation:** Maintain a spotless home, with a perfectly organized pantry and a perpetually clean floor.
**Reality:** Eat off paper plates, find dog hair in unusual places, and consider "tidying up" a win.
**Caption:** "I'll take the win."

## Idea 4
**Expectation:** Be the most patient and understandable friend/partner/parent.
** Reality:** Have a short fuse, make passive-aggressive comments, and hide in the bathroom for 5 minutes of peace.
**Caption:** "I'll take the win."

## Idea 5
**Expectation:** Master a new skill, like playing the guitar or learning a new language.
**Reality:** Start a Duolingo streak, practice guitar for 10 minutes, and then get distracted by a shiny object.
**Caption:** "I'll take the win."

## Idea 6
**Expectation:** Prepare gourmet, home-cooked meals every night.
**Reality:** Reheat leftovers, order takeout, or survive on cereal and toast.
**Caption:** "I'll take the win."

## Voice Notes for Calligraphic Cards

**Key phrases to capture in handwriting:**
- "Hot damn!" – signature exclamation, high energy
- "You are amazing" – direct affirmation
- "I/We're so proud of you" – personal connection
- "Walk this path with you" – community language
- "Apart Not Alone" – brand signature

**Tone markers:**
- Casual, not corporate
- Direct address (you/your)
- Exclamation points for enthusiasm
- Short declarative sentences
- Brief reflection prompts on longer milestones (9mo+)

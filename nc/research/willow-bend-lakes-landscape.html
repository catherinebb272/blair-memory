Half Bath Analysis

10 properties have half baths. No strong correlation with price/sqft. Location and views matter more than extra powder room.

Pricing Strategy

  • Price at/below Zestimate for 0-7 day sale
  • Lake/golf views: Add 20-30% premium
  • Expect longer DOM in fall/winter: Oct-Nov avg 65 days vs spring avg 3 days
  • DOM exceeds 30 days: Consider 5-10% reduction
  • Newer construction (2017+): Justifies significant premium

Key Observations

Acreage doesn't strongly correlate with price/sqft. Year built matters: 2017 = $732/sqft vs 1979-1986 avg = $360/sqft. Bedroom/bath count less important than total sqft and premium features. Most homes sold at/below list — pricing discipline by sellers.

Notable Sales

## Key Findings

### Lake View Premium
- **5128 Seascape (Lake):** $732/sqft — **highest in dataset**
- **2209 Colonial (Golf):** $448/sqft — second highest
- **Premium:** Lake/golf views add **$200-300/sqft** over standard pool homes

### Pricing Insights
- Most homes sold at or below list price
- **5128 Seascape:** New construction (2017) commands massive premium
- **Acreage:** Does not strongly correlate with price/sqft
- **Pool:** Baseline expectation (all comps have pools)

---

## Pricing Strategy Recommendations

1. **Price at or slightly below Zestimate** for 0-7 day sale
2. **Lake/golf views:** Add 20-30% premium
3. **Expect longer DOM in fall/winter:** Oct-Nov avg 65 days vs spring avg 3 days
4. **If DOM exceeds 30 days:** Consider 5-10% price reduction
5. **Newer construction (2017+):** Justifies significant premium regardless of view

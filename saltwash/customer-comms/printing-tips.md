## The full reply (copy-paste as-is, or trim per question)

```
Happy to help. Here's what's in your download and how to get the best result from it.

Your file is a print-ready JPG, sized to the dimensions in the filename. The download includes 5 sizes: 5x7, 8x10, 11x14, 16x24, and 18x24. Open the file you want and send that specific one to your printer — don't resize up or down, just print at the size in the filename. Each file is already at print resolution for its size.

For the paper, go with a matte or linen finish. The piece is built on a watercolor-style texture, and glossy photo paper flattens that out — you lose the depth the painting is meant to have. A heavier matte or linen cardstock (around 80-100 lb / 200-270 gsm) gives you something that feels like the real thing. Skip watercolor paper itself; the tooth of the paper fights the texture already in the image.

For printers: Gelato is the easiest if you want a quality print mailed to you without leaving the house. Upload the file, pick your size and paper, they handle the rest. Printful works but leans toward their own product line, so Gelato is the better fit for a single art print. For a local option, Staples and FedEx Office both do large-format prints and same-day pickup. If you've got a home inkjet that handles cardstock, that works too — just set the paper type to "matte" or "fine art" in the print dialog.

For framing, all the sizes we offer fit standard off-the-shelf frames. The piece has generous white border built in, so you don't need a mat unless you want one.

If you want a different size, the watercolor image is built to handle small crops on the edges. Just let me know what size you're after and I'll let you know if it'll work cleanly.

Let me know how it turns out.
```

## Background (do not send to buyer)

- **Why no watercolor paper:** The image already carries a built-in watercolor texture in the file. Real watercolor paper has a pronounced tooth (surface texture) that competes with the digital texture and makes the result look muddied.
- **Why Gelato over Printful for single prints:** Printful's fulfillment is built around their own branded products (mugs, shirts, etc.). Gelato is print-on-demand art-focused and handles single prints more naturally. Both are real, both work — Gelato is just a better fit.
- **Why home inkjet works:** Modern inkjets with matte/fine-art settings handle 80–100 lb cardstock well. The key is the paper-type setting in the print dialog — wrong setting is what makes home prints look bad, not the printer.

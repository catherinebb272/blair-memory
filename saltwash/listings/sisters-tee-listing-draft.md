## Description

Five women. One watercolor wash. The kind of shirt your whole group will fight over on the night out.

The *Sisters With Me* tee features a soft watercolor study of five fashion-illustration women — loose brushwork, muted coastal palette, the kind of pretty that reads as art, not as loud. Underneath: **"I've got all my sisters with me."**

Made for the moments you want to mark together.

**Why this one works for the whole crew**

- Pairs with anything — denim, slip skirts, joggers
- Art-forward graphic that photographs beautifully (hello, group pics)
- Inclusive silhouette, flattering fit, soft hand-feel
- Easy to dress up or down across an entire weekend

**Perfect for**
- Bachelorette parties (bride tribe, matching group tees)
- Bridal showers and engagement celebrations
- Girls' nights, birthdays, Galentine's
- Girls' trips, beach weekends, sorority events
- A "just because" gift for your ride-or-die

**Product details**
- Soft, lightweight fabric with a comfortable relaxed fit
- High-quality print that holds its color and softness wash after wash
- Available in women's sizes — please use the size chart to find your best fit
- Printed on demand — please allow time for production and shipping

**Care**
- Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors
- Tumble dry low or hang dry
- Do not bleach or iron directly on the print

**The Saltwash note**
Designed in our studio with a love for soft palettes and the people who make any room warmer. Every Saltwash piece starts with a watercolor original — never just a stock graphic.

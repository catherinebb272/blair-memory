## Description

Quiet afternoon on the coast — a book left open on sun-warmed linen, the dunes holding the light. *Beach Read* is a soft watercolor study of the hours that don't need anything from you.

*This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.*

**What you'll receive**
Five high-resolution JPG files at 300 DPI, sized to standard US frame formats:
- 5×7 (1500×2100)
- 8×10 (2400×3000)
- 11×14 (3300×4200)
- 16×24 (4800×7200)
- 18×24 (5400×7200)

**How it's made**
Painted from a place anchor on the Outer Banks, refined and translated into watercolor in our studio. The image is generated with AI assistance, then reworked by hand for the loose wash, the salt-bleached light, the quiet.

**Please note**
Frame not included. Colors may vary slightly between screens and printers.

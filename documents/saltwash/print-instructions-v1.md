# Printing Your Saltwash Art Print

Every Saltwash print is a high-resolution JPG, prepared at 300 DPI for art-quality reproduction. Here's how to get the best result at whatever size you've chosen.

---

## Printing at Home

Most home printers max out at 8×10. If you're printing at home, choose the **5×7** or **8×10** size.

- Set your printer to its **highest quality photo setting**
- Use premium photo paper (matte or gloss) for the truest color and weight
- Make sure "Fit to page" is **off** — you want 1:1 sizing, not scaling

**A note on edges:** Home printers have a small non-printable margin (usually around ¼″ or less), so the very edges of the image may be slightly cropped. The compositions are built to allow for this — nothing important sits right on the border.

---

## For Best Results — Especially Larger Sizes

For 11×14, 16×20, and up, a professional print service will give you noticeably better color, paper weight, and longevity. Two easy routes:

### Local Print Shop
Take the JPG file to **Staples**, **FedEx Office**, or a similar local printer. Most can print 11×14 and up on premium photo or fine art paper, often the same day. Bring the file on a USB drive or upload ahead through their website.

### Online Print Services
- **Gelato** — global print network, strong on art prints, ships locally so delivery is often faster and cheaper than other online printers
- **Printful** — also handles art prints, known for consistent quality, sometimes bundled with framing options

Upload the JPG, choose your size and paper, and order. The file is ready to print at 300 DPI for every size offered.

## File Specs

- **Format:** JPG
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI at all offered sizes (5×7, 8×10, 11×14, 16×20)
- **Color profile:** sRGB — standard for screen viewing and most home printing

## Demographics: Two Very Different Audiences

### Traditional Player
- Older demographic (baby boomers+)
- American NMJL mahjong with yearly card
- Country club, JCC, library groups
- Progress tracking, hands learned, structured play

### New/Social Player
- Younger crowd (20s-40s)
- Discovery via TikTok, social media
- Bar/restaurant mahjong nights (San Francisco leading)
- "Get off phones and socialize" — AP News called it "the new trivia night"
- More flexible on rules, interested in Riichi, house variations
- Crazy Rich Asians (2018) drove major interest
- Eventbrite SF: events up **150% YoY** (2023→2024)

### Key Insight
Different products may appeal to different audiences:
- Traditional: journal/tracker, learning tools, NMJL-focused
- New/Social: party rules app, funky merch, casual learning tools

## Oh My Mahjong - Competitor Profile

### New Discovery: House Rules Book
**"Mahjong: House Rules from Across the Asian Diaspora"** by Nicole Wong
- Published April 2025 by Hardie Grant North America
- $25 hardcover
- **First English-language book to center Asian diaspora rules**
- Started as "The Mahjong Project" (2019) - oral history documenting family's house rules
- Covers: history of game, rules, variations, how to play
- Three different house rules sets documented in Chinese diaspora alone
- Includes cultural context, family stories, "how to trash talk at the mahjong table"
- Bonus: breakdown of mahjong hands in Crazy Rich Asians scene

### Other Books
- "The Complete Book of Mah Jongg" by Amy Lo - Covers American + international variants
- "Mah Jong Handbook" by Tuttle - General reference
- "Mahjong 101 Book" (Oh My Mahjong) - American-focused, mentions house rules variations
- NMJL official rule book

### Print-on-Demand / Merch
- **Mahjong planner stickers** on Etsy (small niche - tile designs for bullet journals)
- No dedicated mahjong planners/journals found
- General print-on-demand platforms: Printify, Printful, Prodigi, Zazzle

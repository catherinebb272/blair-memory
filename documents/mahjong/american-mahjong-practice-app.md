# American Mahjong Practice App - Research

## Overview
- **Developer:** Eight Bam LLC
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Windows
- **Price:** ~$18 one-time for current year hands, or $4/month subscription
- **Founded:** ~10+ years (claimed "Original American Mahjong App")

## Popularity
- **iOS:** 4.9⭐ with 20K ratings
- **Android:** 10K+ downloads, 4.3⭐
- Likely 100K-200K+ lifetime downloads on iOS

## Key Features
- Mobile-first design (large tiles, card displayed under hand)
- **Suggested Hands:** Evaluates all possible hand combinations, sorts by highest matching tile count, shows overlapping hands
- Practice mode: solo or vs bots, filter by card sections, replay/undo
- Daily challenge with leaderboard
- Hand validation and corrections
- NMJL rules alerts

## Companion Tools
- **lookup.eightbam.com** - Tile-to-hand finder
- **defense.eightbam.com** - "What hand are they playing?" defense trainer

## Market Position
Game-centric. Even the lookup tool is a companion to the game, not a standalone learning aid.

## Gap Identified
No dedicated "here's my tile selection, show me what's possible" learning tool that isn't embedded in a game.

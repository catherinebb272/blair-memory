# Oh My Mahjong - Competitor Profile

## Overview
- **Founded:** By Megan Jett Trottier (artist/painter)
- **Mission:** "Spread American Mahjong like confetti"
- **Tagline:** "Modern version of a timeless classic"
- **Positioning:** Tradition meets modern style — vibrant, colorful, gift-worthy

---

## Vibe & Aesthetic
- **Color palette:** Bold, bright, modern patterns
- **Design approach:** Based on traditional tile designs but with modern twist + color
- **Target:** Younger generation, gift-giving market, modern home
- **Founder story:** Based her "OG Line" on original mahjong set given when she graduated college. Second line features bright painted hearts from her "What You Love" artwork series.

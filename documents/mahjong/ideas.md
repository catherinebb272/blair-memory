# Mahjong Business Ideas

## Current Notes
- Gathering research on opportunities in the mahjong space
- Exploring: apps, print-on-demand, house rules, party games, learning tools

---

## Idea: Standalone Tile Learning Tool
**Problem:** No app that says "here's my tiles, show me what hands are possible" without being a game
- American Mahjong Practice has "suggested hands" but embedded in game
- Gap: lightweight reference/learning tool

**Position:** Not a game — a focused reference/learning aid

---

## Idea: House Rules Variant Guide
**Problem:** Scattered info, no central repo for fun/crazy variations
- Nicole Wong's book (2025) covers diaspora rules — closest existing
- Reddit has party ideas but nothing organized

**Formats could be:**
- PDF/downloadable guide
- Card deck of "house rules" to try
- App with random rule generator for game night

---

## Idea: Mahjong Game Tracker / Planner Journal
**Problem:** No dedicated planner for tracking games, scores, hands learned, progress
- Etsy has stickers (small niche)
- Print-on-demand space wide open
- Could track: hands completed, win rate, tiles to study, game nights

**Formats:**
- Print-on-demand journal (Printify/Printful)
- Digital planner (GoodNotes, Notion)
- Hybrid with physical + QR codes to digital stats

---

## Idea: "Party Mahjong" Variant App
**Problem:** People want fun/crazy variations for game nights but no easy way to try them
- Mix Riichi + American rules
- Random rule generator ("tonight's special rules")
- Could be a companion for game night hosts

---

## Idea: Beginner→Advanced Structured Learning Path
**Problem:** No clear progression for learning
- Current resources are either "learn basics" or "strategy for experts"
- Could be: online course, PDF guide, app with levels

---

## Idea: Mahjong Tile Accessorizing
- Custom tile cases, bags, mats
- Personalized sets
- This is more physical product - different skillset

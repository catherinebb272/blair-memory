# Launchpad Image Descriptions

## Canva Visual Descriptions

**1. Hello (Hero)**
- Astronaut raccoon floating in zero-g, small spaceship in background, Earth rising over shoulder. Deep void bg with cyan stars.

**2. Names**
- Raccoon at retro control console, typing on keyboard. Starfield bg with faint nebula. Cyan accent glow on screen.

**3. Use Cases**
- Raccoon holding a mission pad/clipboard with checkmarks. Satellite dish in foreground. Dark bg with data streams.

**4. Personality**
- Swirling nebula in violet/cyan/pink. No raccoon — pure space atmosphere. Subtle star points.

**5. Communication**
- Concentric signal waves radiating outward (cyan). Raccoon optional in corner. Clean, minimal.

**6. Model**
- Raccoon adjusting knobs on a wall panel. Gears and circuit lines visible. Techy, warm lighting.

**7. Integrations**
- Abstract node network — connected dots in cyan, lines weaving. No raccoon. Web/tangle aesthetic.

**8. Memory**
- Floating cubes in cyan/violet, each with faint glow. Like data crystals. Raccoon reaching toward one.

**9. Working Hours**
- Vintage hourglass with cyan sand. Clock gears in background. Warm amber accent.

**10. Privacy**
- Balance scale (cyan left, violet right). Shield icon. No raccoon — official/secure feel.

**11. Finish**
- Raccoon in pilot seat, hand on launch button. Flames exiting frame. High energy, ready to go.

**12. Mobile Avatar**
- Just the Heyron logo (H in hexagon) — tiny raccoon head peeking. Clean, favicon-style.

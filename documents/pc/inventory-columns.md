# Plato's Closet Inventory Data Column Definitions

- **Column G** (Prod Adjust Date): Date something about the product was changed (e.g., price inside the computer)
- **Column H**: Date someone adjusted the quantity
- **Column I** (Adjustment Qty): Amount the quantity was changed
  - `-1` = written off
  - `1` = added back in

Used for identifying large write-off events.

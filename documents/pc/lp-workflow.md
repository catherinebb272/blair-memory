*A step-by-step framework for Plato's Closet franchise owners*

## Phase 1: Initial Conversation

### Step 1: Ask the Owner These Questions

Before analyzing any data, understand the owner's perspective:

1. **What do you think is happening?** (Suspected cause: shoplifting, employee theft, fake buys?)

2. **How long have you noticed the problem?** 

3. **Do you have any suspects?** (Who has warehouse key? Who handles write-offs?)

4. **What's your gut feel on the $ amount?**

5. **Have you done any manual "digital inventory counts"?** (When?)

6. **Do you have security cameras?** (Footage from which periods available?)

7. **Has anyone left recently?** (Employment dates for suspects)

---

## Phase 2: Data Collection

### Step 2: Request These Reports from DRS

Ask the owner to export these four reports for a date range (recommend 12 months):

| Report | What It Shows | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| **Item Buy Detail** | All buy transactions, sale dates, costs, adjustments | Main data for analysis |
| **Inventory Adjustments** | Who adjusted what, when, reason code | Identifies WHO is doing write-offs |
| **Buyer Evaluation** | Who opened/closed buys, price changes, who sold items | Catches fake buys & price manipulation |
| **Period Close** | Sales, returns, shrinkage by period | Return rate should be 1-2% |

**File format:** CSV or Excel

---

## Phase 3: Upload & Analysis

### Step 3: Upload Reports to Analysis Tool

The owner should upload:
1. Item Buy Detail CSV
2. Inventory Adjustments (if available)
3. Buyer Evaluation (if available)
4. Period Close (if available)

### Step 4: Run Analysis Checks

#### Check A: Write-Off Analysis
- Pull top write-off dates
- Match to employment timeline
- Identify WHO did the write-offs

#### Check B: Suspicious Transactions
- Find 3+ item transactions with ZERO sales (full fake buys)
- Find 5+ item transactions where 2+ items at END never sold (intermittent)
- Rank by buyer

#### Check C: Inventory Gap
- DRS computer value - Physical inventory value
- Calculate missing bins/pieces/$

#### Check D: Price Analysis
- Group unsold items by price range
- Identify if theft targets cheap junk or expensive items

#### Check E: Return Rate (Period Close)
- Calculate return rate = returns / sales
- Normal: 1-2%
- Elevated = possible fake returns

---

## Phase 4: Review Findings

### Step 5: Discuss Results with Owner

Key questions to resolve:

1. **Does this match your suspicion?** (Confirm suspect)

2. **Do the write-off dates align with who you think was doing them?** (Verify from Inventory Adjustments)

3. **Are the suspicious transactions from the right people?** (Check employment dates)

4. **Is the return rate elevated?** (Check Period Close)

5. **What's the most likely theft vector?** (Fake buys, self-write-offs, fake returns, physical theft?)

---

## Phase 5: Action Plan

### Step 6: Recommend Next Steps

Based on findings, one or more of:

- **Confront suspect** with evidence
- **Review security footage** from specific dates
- **Audit inventory** (physical scan)
- **Check cash drawer** for discrepancies
- **Monitor future transactions** for patterns
- **File police report** if warranted

---

## Theft Vectors Covered

1. **Classic Fake Buys** — Enter items under fake name, never existed
2. **Intermittent Fake Items** — Add 1-3 items after customer leaves
3. **Personal/Goodwill Donations** — Bring own junk to "sell"
4. **Self-Write-Offs** — Write off own fake buys to cover tracks
5. **Fake Returns** — Process fake return, pocket cash
6. **Physical Theft** — Walking out with inventory (key holder)

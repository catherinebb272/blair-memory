Mini Cycle‑Count Helper

This tool walks you through the inventory reconciliation workflow used by ANA.

  • Upload Item Buy Detail (Excel) and Precount (CSV)
  • Upload the physical‑count scan file
  • Review “missing” items – accept or skip
  • Download a CSV of items to write‑off (mandatory)
  • Optional barcode generation step

FAQ & Security

Q: Can I export the whole session?
A: Use your browser’s “Save page as…” to keep a copy.

### Key Findings

1. **Write-offs were skewing the data** — Nearly half of what appeared as "unsold inventory" was actually items written off. When excluded, the unsold rates drop from 7-8% down to 3-6% — a much healthier picture.

2. **Top 3 buyers still have exposure** — Katelyn Fipps, Amber Digerolamo, and Peyton Penrose have the highest absolute numbers of unsold items (excluding write-offs), but their percentages are now in the 4.7-5.0% range.

3. **Best performers** — Gohar Kirakosyan (2.8%), Trainee (2.6%), and Valentina Ruiz (3.1%) have the lowest unsold rates — these buyers are moving inventory most effectively.

4. **Needs attention** — Gregory Lebras (6.8%), Mariel Tovia(s) (6.6%), and Ashley Childress (6.0%) have the highest unsold percentages. Not dramatically high, but notably above average.

5. **Dollar exposure** — The top 3 buyers by unsold cost (Katelyn, Amber, Peyton) account for $8,487.14 in genuinely unsold inventory — still significant, but down from $12K when write-offs were included.

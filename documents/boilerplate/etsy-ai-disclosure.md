# Etsy AI Disclosure — Boilerplate

Two standard sentences for AI-assisted artwork disclosure. One per shop. Use the version that fits the shop; don't mix.

---

## Standard sentences

### Saltwash Prints (use for all Saltwash listings)

> Each Saltwash print begins with our original photography and is reimagined using AI-assisted rendering, then refined and prepared for print by us.

### Apart Not Alone (use for all ANA listings — coins, cards, etc.)

> This artwork was created from our original design concepts and prompts using AI-assisted design tools, then refined, edited, color-corrected, and prepared for print by us.

---

## Why two sentences, not one

The two shops have different relationships to AI:

- **Saltwash** is built on Catherine's original photography → AI reimagining → print. The disclosure has to start with the photography (otherwise the AI sounds like the source, not a step in the chain).
- **ANA** products start from design concepts and prompts, not photography. The disclosure has to mention color-correction and editing because coin/card work involves more post-processing than Saltwash's print pipeline.

If you ever launch a third shop, write a new sentence that matches that shop's pipeline. Don't reuse either of these as templates — the voice and pipeline-specific verbs matter.

---

## Where to drop it

Standard Etsy placement (per general Etsy guidance + what the platforms look for):

### Shop-level (recommended for both shops)
- **About section** of the Etsy shop. Visible on the shop homepage. Sets the brand-level expectation.
- **Shop banner / Story** if it fits the layout.

### Per-listing (recommended for both shops)
- **Description** — top is more visible, bottom is less disruptive to the buying flow. Pick one and stay consistent within a line.
- **Etsy's "AI generation" disclosure field** if/when a specific listing falls under Etsy's structured AI disclosure rules. Check the listing-edit UI each time you upload; Etsy has been expanding this area and the rules change.

### Where it does NOT belong
- Buried in image alt text (buyers don't see it; platforms may not scan it for disclosure)
- Behind a "click for details" link
- Inside the digital download file itself (some buyers will see it, but many won't)

---

## Customization per listing

If a specific listing uses AI *differently* than the default for that shop — e.g., a piece that's hand-painted with minimal AI touch-up, or one that's fully AI-rendered and reviewed — the per-listing description should call that out explicitly:

- "This piece was hand-painted by our studio artist and only minimally color-corrected digitally."
- "This piece is fully AI-rendered and reviewed by hand before print."

Don't apply the boilerplate to pieces where it isn't true. If a listing has no AI involvement, it doesn't need the disclosure.

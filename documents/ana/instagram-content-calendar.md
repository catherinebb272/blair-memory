# ANA Instagram Content Calendar

**Status:** Planning phase  
**Created:** 2026-05-10  
**Owner:** Barbara + Catherine (Blair can help with drafting)

---

## Strategy

- 1 new milestone post every 2 weeks
- Mix of carousel + reel formats
- Stories in between for engagement
- Continuous content from 12 coin milestones
- Tone: youthful, edgy, optimistic, realistic

---

## Content Series: 12 Milestones

| Week | Milestone | Post Type | Theme/Vibe |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | Month 1 | Carousel | "Day 30 — you made it." Fresh, excited, "new beginning" energy |
| 2 | Month 2 | Reel | "60 days strong" — routine building, what's changed |
| 3 | Month 3 | Carousel | "Quarter check-in" — reflection, first big milestone feeling |
| 4 | Month 4 | Story (poll/question) | Engagement: "What's the hardest part for you?" |
| 5 | Month 5 | Carousel | "Almost at the half-year" — building momentum |
| 6 | Month 6 | Reel | **HALF YEAR** — big celebration, coin reveal |
| 7 | Month 9 | Carousel | "Almost to year one" — reflection on growth |
| 8 | 1 Year Koi | Reel | Gold coin debut. Koi symbolism: resilience, swimming upstream |
| 9 | 1 Year Phoenix | Carousel | Phoenix rising — transformation theme |
| 10 | 18 Month | Story (quiz) | Engagement: "Guess what's next?" |
| 11 | 2 Year | Reel | Major milestone — community, longevity |
| 12 | 3 Year | Carousel | Platinum finale — the full journey |

---

## Between Milestones (Filler/Engagement)

- **Week 2, 4, 6, etc.:** Quote graphics, "real talk" text posts, community stories
- **Post formats to rotate:** Carousel (education), Reel (coin reveals/motion), Story (polls, Q&A)
- **1 CTA per post** — "link in bio" or "DM us"

---

## Production Notes

**Tools:**
- Canva (primary design + animation)
- MidJourney or Adobe Firefly for AI imagery
- Barbara's coin art as hero assets

**Animation approach:**
- Coin-centric (coins animate, no founders on camera)
- 15-30 second loops
- Carousel-optimized for slide-by-slide

**Batch production:** Create all 12, then schedule drip-feed

---

## Timeline

- **2-week pace:** 24 weeks (6 months) of content
- **After Series 1:** Update imagery, add customer spotlights, repeat

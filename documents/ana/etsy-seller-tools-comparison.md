# Etsy Seller Tools Comparison

*Research date: May 2026*

---

## Section 1: Print on Demand Design Tools

---

## Kittl

**Pricing:**
- Free: $0 – 5 projects, 200 AI tokens (one-time)
- Pro: $12/mo (billed annually) – unlimited projects, 2,000 AI tokens/mo
- Expert: $26/mo – unlimited, 6,000 AI tokens/mo, 100GB storage

**Key Features:**
- AI image generation (DALL-E, Flux, Ideogram, GPT Image)
- Built for merchandise (t-shirts, hoodies, stickers, mockups)
- Professional templates + mockups
- Vector tools
- Commercial license on paid plans

**Pros:**
- Strong multi-model AI generation
- POD-specific (mockups, merch templates)
- Unlimited projects on paid plans
- Commercial use included

**Cons:**
- AI tokens can run out fast
- Learning curve if new to design tools
- Less established than Adobe

---

## Playground AI (playground.com)

**Pricing:**
- Free: $0 – 10 images/3 hrs, non-commercial
- Pro: $12/mo – 75 images/3 hrs, 150 credits/mo, 2K editing
- Pro Plus: $36/mo – unlimited generations, 1,000 credits/mo, 4K

**Key Features:**
- AI image generation (GPT Image 2, Nano Banana, Seedream)
- Templates for social media, logos, t-shirts
- Upscaling & background removal (paid)
- SVG exports

**Pros:**
- Strong AI image generation
- Commercial license on Pro+
- You own what you create

**Cons:**
- Free tier very limited (non-commercial)
- Not as POD-focused as Kittl
- Credit system can be confusing

---

## Adobe Express

**Pricing:**
- Free: Very limited
- Premium: ~$9.99/mo (often bundled with Creative Cloud)

**Key Features:**
- Firefly AI (commercially safe)
- Huge template library
- Adobe Stock integration
- Brand consistency tools
- Social media scheduling
- Works with Photoshop/Illustrator

**Pros:**
- Most versatile platform
- Adobe ecosystem integration
- Massive template library
- Commercial use with premium

**Cons:**
- Free tier very limited
- Can feel bloated
- AI not as robust as Kittl/Playground

---

## Section 2: Keyword & Ranking Tools for Etsy

---

## eRank

**Pricing:**
- Free: $0 – 5 keyword searches/day, 1 shop, 50 listings
- Basic: ~$10-15/mo – 100 searches/day, 5 shops
- Pro/Expert: Higher tiers for more shops/searches

**Key Features:**
- Keyword research with trend data (Etsy, Amazon, eBay)
- Competitor tracking (track estimated daily sales)
- Shop audits & listing audits
- AI Listing Helper (generate optimized titles, tags, descriptions)
- Browser extension (works on Etsy, Printify, Shopify)
- Spotted on Etsy monitoring (top 100 rankings)

**Pros:**
- 2M+ sellers trust it – most established
- Strong free tier to start
- Works across multiple marketplaces
- Good browser extension

**Cons:**
- Gets pricey at higher tiers
- Some features locked behind Expert

---

## RankHero

**Pricing:**
- Free: $0 – 3 uses/day across tools
- Side Hustle: $7.99/mo – 25 uses/day, 3 shops
- Business: $12.99/mo – 100 uses/day, 10 shops, all tools unlocked
- Enterprise: $49.99/mo – 300 uses/day, 25 shops

**Key Features:**
- Niche Finder
- Shop Analyzer & Listing Analyzer
- Keyword Generator, Title Generator, Tag Generator
- Rank Checker
- Profit Calculator
- RankHero AI (50-100 uses/day on paid plans)
- Video Generator for listings

**Pros:**
- Budget-friendly ($7.99/mo entry)
- All-in-one AI tools (titles, tags, descriptions)
- Video generator for listings
- 500K+ users

**Cons:**
- Newer platform (less established trust)
- Free tier very limited (3 uses/day)

---

## Alura

**Pricing:** Free + paid tiers (Growth: ~$20-30/mo, Business: ~$50+/mo)

**Key Features:**
- Keyword & Product Research (find winning products)
- Shop Operations & Finances (listing recommendations, financial reports)
- Marketing & Automations (email marketing, Pinterest auto-posting)
- AI Listing Optimization (write titles/descriptions, Tag Insights)
- A/B Testing (thumbnails, titles, prices)
- Etsy Ads Management (auto-optimize ad spend)
- Privacy Policy Generator
- 800K+ users

**Pros:**
- Most comprehensive – keywords + ops + marketing
- Built-in email marketing & Pinterest automation
- AI listing optimization + A/B testing
- Profit calculator & shop analytics

**Cons:**
- Can feel overwhelming if you just need keyword research
- Full suite is pricier
- Learning curve higher

---

## EverBee

**Pricing:**
- Hobby: $0 – 10 keywords/day, 5 favorites folders
- Growth: $19.99/mo (billed $239/yr) – unlimited keywords, trends
- Business: $69/mo (billed $828/yr) – 24-month historical data

**Key Features:**
- Keyword Score (built-in ranking metric)
- Product Analytics (sales, revenue, conversion rate)
- Trends & Growth Rate (track from last month to 24 months)
- Tag Analyzer
- Email marketing built in
- Advanced filters

**Pros:**
- Keyword Score makes evaluation easy
- Strong trend tracking (up to 24 months)
- Product analytics with conversion data
- Good free tier

**Cons:**
- Business plan is pricey ($69/mo)
- Less well-known than eRank

## For ANA (Apart Not Alone)

All four tools could help with:
- Finding relevant keywords for sobriety milestone coins
- Tracking competitor shops
- Optimizing listing titles/tags
- Identifying trending products in self-care/gift markets

**My suggestion:** Start with eRank's free tier to test, or RankHero at $7.99/mo if you want the AI generation tools. Alura if you want to go all-in-one. EverBee if conversion data matters more than keywords.

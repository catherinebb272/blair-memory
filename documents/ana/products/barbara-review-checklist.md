## How to use this checklist

1. **Print one copy per product line** in the box. (Softer side = copy 1, Spoons = copy 2, etc.)
2. **Mark directly on the cards/stickers** with sticky notes if you want — keep the physical reaction.
3. **Transfer notes here** at the end, so Catherine can read in 10 minutes.
4. **Skip what doesn't apply** — not every section is required for every line.
5. **Send back via email/Discord** with photos of marked-up cards if that's easier than transcribing.

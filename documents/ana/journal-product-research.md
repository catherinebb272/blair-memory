# ANA Sobriety Journal Research

**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Purpose:** Evaluate sobriety journals as potential product line extension (digital + print) for Apart Not Alone.
**Status:** Research summary — to inform product decision.

---

## Market Snapshot

The sobriety journal category is mature and crowded on Amazon, with ~$13–$30 price points for guided print journals. The digital/printable category on Etsy is active but undifferentiated — most listings are generic "recovery workbook" templates with templated prompts, often 50–200 pages of fillable PDFs in 8.5x11" format.

**ANA's positioning opportunity:** Secular. Not AA, not 12-step, not religious, not gendered. This is an underserved niche — most existing journals are either explicitly Christian/12-step OR explicitly "for women" (Dear Alcohol, Sober as Fuck, etc.) OR both.

---

## What People Actually Use (by feature frequency)

Based on reviews across Clever Fox, BookFactory, "Dear Alcohol," "Let That Sh*t Go," "Sober on a Drunk Planet," Sobriety Journal by William Porter, and Reddit threads on r/stopdrinking and r/SoberCurious:

### Core / near-universal features
- **Daily reflection prompts** (every major journal has these)
- **Gratitude lists** (typically 3 items/day)
- **Mood check-in** (1–10 scale or emoji)
- **Tracking space for sober days** (counter, calendar, or chain)
- **Free-write space** for stream-of-consciousness
- **Trigger identification** — what set off cravings and how they responded

### Highly valued additions
- **Weekly review / progress check** (most do this)
- **Goal-setting pages** (short + long term)
- **"Wins" or "victories" log** (counterweights to negative focus)
- **Affirmations / quotes page** (some journals open with a daily quote)
- **Habit tracker** (sleep, exercise, water, meetings)

### Differentiating features (rare, but loved)
- **Milestone celebrations built-in** — pages that mark Day 30, 90, 6mo, 1yr with reflection prompts
- **Letter-to-self** prompts for future sobriety date
- **Memory/keepsake pages** — places to glue ticket stubs, sobriety coins, meeting chips
- **"Why I quit" / origin story page** (often near the front)
- **Coping toolkit list** — what works when craving hits
- **Accountability partner check-in space**

## Common Complaints (what people want but don't get)

- **Too little writing space** — biggest complaint, mentioned in 60%+ of reviews
- **Repetitive prompts** — "Day 47 looks the same as Day 3"
- **Too much "dear alcohol" / gendered language** (pushes away men, non-binary, secular folks)
- **Religious/12-step framing** (turns off secular users — "higher power," "surrender," "steps")
- **No tracking component** (notebooks-with-prompts without structure feel directionless)
- **No milestone recognition** (gets to Day 90, journal is "done" with no celebration)
- **Cheap paper / binding** (gel pen bleed, falls apart by month 2)
- **Pretty cover, shallow content** (decoration over substance)

## Digital / Printable (Etsy market)

- $3–$12 typical price
- Most are generic Canva templates repurposed for sobriety
- Top sellers bundle: daily check-in + gratitude + mood + habit tracker (20–80 pages)
- Top opportunity gaps in digital:
  - **Secular (not AA / not religious)**
  - **Structured around milestones (30, 60, 90, 6mo, 1yr)**
  - **Phone-friendly / tablet-fillable (GoodNotes, Notability)**
  - **Coordinating with physical milestones** (e.g., coin journaling pages)

## Recommendations for ANA

If Catherine/Barbara/Drew want to test journals, three viable directions:

1. **The ANA-branded secular sobriety journal (print + digital bundle)**
   - Lean into secular/non-12-step positioning (major underserved angle)
   - 90-day or 6-month structure aligned with their coin milestones
   - Includes "milestone pages" for each coin they sell (clever cross-sell)
   - Pages for the recipient to journal *about* getting a coin / giving a coin

2. **Companion journal pages for coin recipients (digital, low cost)**
   - Sold as a $3–5 PDF add-on for any coin purchase
   - "How I earned my [6-month] coin" reflection pages
   - 12-page pack keyed to the 12 milestones
   - Upsell mechanism for existing coin buyers

3. **Recovery milestone journal (analog to coins)**
   - 12-section journal, one per milestone
   - Each section opens with the coin's symbolism, then reflection prompts
   - Premium product ($25–35), physical print
   - Clear moat: nobody else is doing milestone-tied journaling

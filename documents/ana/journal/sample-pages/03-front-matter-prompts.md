# Sample Page 3 — Front Matter (Introductory Pages)

**Use case:** First ~12 pages of the book, before the daily pages start. Sets tone, captures origin, names accountability, opens with a quote.

There are 7 distinct front-matter pages. Generate each as a separate ChatGPT mockup. Use the same Style Tokens block from `README.md` at the top of every prompt.

---

## Page FM-1 — Inside Cover / Ownership Page

**ChatGPT prompt:**

A single 6"x9" interior book page. Centered vertically and horizontally. Content, top to bottom:

- Tiny centered mark at top (0.5 inch from top): the ANA logo (two fish in a circle, line drawing), 0.6 inch
- 0.5 inch of breathing space
- Centered, in a clean serif, 18pt, deep forest green, with generous letter-spacing: `this journal belongs to`
- 1 inch of breathing space
- A single long horizontal rule, taking up 60% of the page width, centered
- 0.75 inch of breathing space
- Same serif, 12pt, warm charcoal, two stacked labels with rules beneath:
  - `started __________________` (date)
  - `will read again __________________` (date one year from start)
- 1 inch of breathing space
- Same serif, 10pt, warm charcoal, italic, centered: `may these pages hold what matters.`

Footer: nothing. The page is a quiet title page.

Render as a top-down flat-lay mockup. Photographic, even daylight, no hands.

The page should feel like the first line of a letter to yourself. Lots of writing room. No other text on the page.

## Page FM-6 — Letter to Day-365 Self

**ChatGPT prompt:**

A single 6"x9" interior book page. Layout — this is a two-part page (a different feel from the other front-matter pages):

- Top-left, small caps, deep forest green, 9pt: `A LETTER TO ME ON DAY 365`
- Thin pencil-gray horizontal rule across full page width
- Below the rule, centered, 11pt serif italic in warm charcoal: `write this now. seal the page. open it in a year.`
- 0.3 inch of breathing space
- Then two visual sections, stacked vertically:
  - Upper half: faint label `FROM ME, ON DAY 1` (small caps, deep forest green, 8pt, top-left of the section), with free-write area (faint rules, full width) taking up the upper 3 inches
  - Lower half: faint label `TO OPEN ON DAY 365` (small caps, deep forest green, 8pt, top-left of the section), with free-write area (faint rules, full width) taking up the lower 3 inches
- Bottom-right, muted ochre italic, 8pt: `be kind to the person reading this.`
- ANA logo bottom-right corner, 0.4 inch

The two-section split is the page's signature — the user writes two letters in one sitting, one to their past (read now, never reread) and one to their future (sealed, opened at 1 year).

Render as a top-down flat-lay mockup. Photographic, even daylight, no hands.

## Page FM-7 — Opening Quote (the Gaiman full-page quote)

**ChatGPT prompt:**

A single 6"x9" interior book page. This is the visual heart of the front matter — a full-bleed typographic moment.

- Background: warm cream, full page
- Centered vertically and horizontally, a single block of text:
  - 18pt serif italic, deep forest green, generous line-height (1.6×): `Write your story`
  - next line, same style: `as it needs to be written.`
- 0.5 inch below the quote, centered, in 10pt small caps warm charcoal, with wide letter-spacing: `— NEIL GAIMAN`
- 1.5 inch below the attribution, centered, in 9pt small caps muted ochre: `1 MONTH MILESTONE QUOTE`
- 0.5 inch above the bottom edge, centered: a single small line-art mark — the torii gate from the 1-month coin, redrawn as a single-weight line drawing, 0.6 inch wide, in deep forest green
- No ANA logo on this page (the torii mark is the visual signature of this spread)
- No header. No footer. No page number.

The page should feel like a deep breath. Almost empty. Just the quote, the attribution, and the gate.

Render as a top-down flat-lay mockup. Photographic, even daylight, no hands.

## Production Notes for Catherine

- These 7 pages can be generated as one batch in ChatGPT (single conversation, 7 outputs) for style consistency. If ChatGPT drifts, regenerate the one that drifted with a tighter style-token block.
- When rendering in Canva, FM-1, FM-4, and FM-7 are the most visually distinctive — those are the three to photograph for the Etsy listing.
- FM-7 (the Gaiman quote) is the one that gets shared on Instagram as a teaser. Plan a square crop version of just the quote + attribution.
- The "letter to day-365" page (FM-6) is the unique selling point. The two-section split is the move. Don't simplify it to a single free-write page.

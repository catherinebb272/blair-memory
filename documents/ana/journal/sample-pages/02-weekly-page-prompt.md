# Sample Page 2 — Weekly Recap Page

**Use case:** 40 of these in the book (weeks 13–52 of sobriety). One per week. Same layout every page.

**ChatGPT prompt — copy everything below this line into ChatGPT:**

---

[PASTE STYLE TOKENS BLOCK FROM `README.md` HERE FIRST]

Now design a single 6"x9" interior book page with this exact layout, top to bottom:

**Header band (top 1 inch):**
- Centered, larger than other labels but still restrained: `WEEK OF ___________________`
- Below that, a small centered italic hint in muted ochre: `(this week's start date — Monday or Sunday, your call)`
- Thin pencil-gray horizontal rule across full page width below the hint

**Section A — Quick stats (1.5 inches tall, full width):**
- Three columns, evenly spaced:
  - Column 1: small caps label `SOBER DAY COUNT`, with a single thick underline below for writing a number
  - Column 2: small caps label `MEETINGS ATTENDED`, with a single thick underline below
  - Column 3: small caps label `DAYS I MOVED MY BODY`, with a single thick underline below

**Section B — Six reflection questions, one per line (4 inches tall, full width):**
Each question is a small-caps label in deep forest green, followed by 2–3 lines of writing space (faint pencil-gray rules).

1. `THIS WEEK'S BIGGEST WIN`
2. `THIS WEEK'S HARDEST MOMENT`
3. `A TRIGGER I NAVIGATED`
4. `SOMEONE I LEANED ON`
5. `WHAT I WANT NEXT WEEK`
6. `A SMALL THING I'M PROUD OF`

Each section has the label, then 2 thin horizontal rules beneath it for writing. The space between sections is about 0.4 inches — generous.

**Section C — Free write (bottom 2 inches of page):**
- Section label, small caps: `OTHER NOTES`
- Lined area, full width, about 6 lines of writing room

**Footer (bottom 0.5 inch):**
- Bottom-left, small caps in muted ochre italic: `carry what you need. leave the rest.`
- Bottom-right: ANA logo mark (two fish in circle, line drawing), 0.4 inch

**Whitespace rules:**
- Same as daily page — generous, calm, never crowded
- The question labels in Section B should feel like a kind friend asking, not a checklist

Render this as a top-down flat-lay mockup on a slightly darker cream background. Photographic, even daylight, no hands, no props, no shadows.

---

**Notes for Catherine when she renders in Canva:**
- Six questions fit because each only needs 2 writing lines. Don't add more lines — constraint = better reflection
- The three-column header (Section A) is the only place with horizontal "underline" style fill-in lines. Everywhere else is full-width rule lines
- The footer italic `carry what you need. leave the rest.` is the only "extra" copy on the page. It's intentional — gives the page a closing tone. Can be swapped for any short supportive phrase, but keep it secular
- The weekly pages are intentionally less structured than the daily pages — the daily pages are a ritual, the weekly pages are a checkpoint

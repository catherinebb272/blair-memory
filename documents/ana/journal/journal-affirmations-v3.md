## 1. Rules of the road

- **5–10 words each.** Short, pithy, fits the affirmation box.
- **Not profound.** These are not poetry or therapy lines. They're small daily nudges.
- **Secular.** No Higher Power language. No "God", "universe", "the light", etc.
- **Broad audience.** Mental health, anxiety, addiction, grief, divorce, identity — not just sobriety.
- **Not gendered.** No "man" / "woman" / "he" / "she". Address the reader directly.
- **One snark per cycle (12 total).** To cut sappiness. Snark flagged with **[snark]**.
- **No duplicate phrases** across all 84.

## 6. How this maps to the merge CSV

The `affirmation` column of `journal-merge-v3.csv` is populated cycle-by-cycle, day 1 → day 7. Snark is not flagged in the CSV — the renderer doesn't need to know. The snark is a content/voice decision, not a layout one.

When Barbara or Catherine revise, the workflow is:

1. Edit this `.md` (the human-readable source)
2. Re-export to CSV (script TBD, but a one-line `awk` or hand-paste handles it for 84 rows)
3. The renderer picks up the new affirmation column automatically

This keeps proofreading in Markdown (where comments, suggestions, and redlines are easy) and production in CSV (where the renderer can read it).

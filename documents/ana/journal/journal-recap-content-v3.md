## 1. Cycle → art → prompt (canonical order)

Each entry has three parts:

- **Image** — what Barbara renders on the left page of the recap spread. Full-bleed watercolor, single accent color, lots of negative space. Mood notes included.
- **Significance** — the short text box overlay on the left page, beneath or beside the art. Tells the reader what the image means in this cycle. 1–2 sentences.
- **Prompt** — the *first* of two recap prompts for this cycle. Tied to the art's meaning. The second prompt will be authored separately (see §Open questions).

**Tone rules (carried from `journal-affirmations-v3.md` §1):** secular, not gendered, no Higher Power, no "you got this," no recovery jargon. Speaks to someone rebuilding, not someone performing.

### Cycle 1 — Torii (1 month coin · new journey, threshold, the start)

**Image.** A single torii gate, vertical posts anchored in soft ground, crossbeam crowning the top. Ocean Indigo line work over a Soft Linen wash, with a soft Golden Sunrise tint blooming behind the gate like a quiet sunrise. No path leads away — the viewer stands in front of it, about to walk through. Negative space dominates. Mood: still, certain, the moment before.

**Significance.** A torii marks the threshold between the everyday and the intentional. You didn't drift through it — you walked. The first month is your own torii: the moment the path stopped being default and became a choice.

**Prompt.** *What threshold did you cross to begin? What did you leave on the other side of the gate?*

---

### Cycle 2 — Waterfall & Lily (18 month coin · flow, ongoing)

**Image.** A waterfall descends in soft vertical washes of Soft Periwinkle, broken by negative space at the base. A single white lily sits in a still pool at the foot of the falls, anchored, undisturbed by the current above. The waterfall is suggested more than drawn — motion through absence. Mood: ongoing, calm, mid-flow.

**Significance.** The water keeps moving. The lily stays put. By cycle 2, you've stopped fighting the current — you're in it. The lily doesn't resist the waterfall; it sits in the water that's been there all along.

**Prompt.** *What have you stopped fighting? Where can you let the current move, while you stay anchored?*

---

### Cycle 3 — Koi, 1-Year variant (1 Year Koi coin · replaces ANA logo in recap rotation per Option A)

**Image.** A single large koi, scaled and luminous, swimming upward through a Soft Sky wash. The koi is gold-toned — leaf-green-gold gradient, scales suggested with delicate line work. Calm water, slow angle of body — moving up, not leaping. A second, smaller koi shadow follows behind. Portrait-oriented to fit the left page. Mood: persistence earned, mature, the long swim.

**Significance.** By the one-year mark, the koi isn't a beginner. It's the koi who's already leapt the falls. The leap is behind it — what matters now is the long swim, not the next jump.

**Prompt.** *What's behind you now? What does the long, steady swim feel like — and how is it different from the leap?*

> **Note for Barbara.** This is the **1-Year Koi** coin (gold tier, ANA catalog #1511799649). Must be visually distinct from the 6mo koi in C11 (below). Suggested differentiation: C3 = portrait composition, single large koi, calm water, gold-leaf palette, body angled slowly upward. C11 = landscape composition, smaller fish in active current, traditional red-orange palette, mid-leap frozen motion. Two different koi watercolors, not one reused.

### Cycle 4 — Stag (4 month coin · quiet strength, grounding, mid-journey)

**Image.** A stag standing in a forest at dusk, head turned slightly toward the viewer. Ocean Indigo line work against a deep Soft Periwinkle wash that fades to Soft Linen at the edges. Antlers branch high. The stag is not posed heroically — simply present, watchful, on its own ground. Mood: quiet, rooted.

**Significance.** The stag doesn't announce itself. It stands its ground, and the ground holds. Four months in, quiet presence is the work — not performance, not declaration. Just standing where you are.

**Prompt.** *Where can you stop performing your strength? What does it feel like to just stand your ground, quietly, for a while?*

---

### Cycle 5 — Kraken Lighthouse (5 month coin · beacon to safe haven + beware lurking perils, mid-journey)

**Image.** A lighthouse on a small rocky outcrop, its beam cutting sideways across the composition. Below, suggested rather than drawn, a single dark tentacle curls around the base of the rocks. Sea is Soft Periwinkle wash; beam is Soft Butter wash. The kraken is implied — the threat is atmospheric, not literal. Mood: vigilance without panic, safe haven and peril in the same frame.

**Significance.** The lighthouse is a beacon — it guides you to safe haven. But beware the kraken: the perils are still in the water. Both are true at once. The light shows the way; the dark is still real. By cycle 5, you've learned to do both: trust the beacon, keep your eyes open.

**Prompt.** *What beacon has guided you to safe haven? What peril are you still watching for — and how do you hold both at once?*

> **Note for Barbara.** The kraken-lighthouse composition carries a specific duality per Catherine (2026-06-08): the lighthouse as beacon *and* the kraken as peril-to-avoid. Both should read in the image. The threat should still feel atmospheric rather than literal — a single dark shape below the surface is enough; the lighthouse's beam is the louder note. If the threat reads too comic-book in watercolor, lean quieter: just the lighthouse, and a single dark mass far below the surface, almost abstract. Catherine's call.

### Cycle 6 — Fox in Snow (9 month coin · solitude, wisdom, the long middle)

**Image.** A single fox mid-stride through deep snow, body low, tail balanced. Soft Linen is the snow itself — white space dominates. The fox is rendered in warm Sunrise Coral line work, the only warmth in the frame. Background fades to white. Mood: solitude, the long middle, weather not yet turned.

**Significance.** The fox doesn't wait for the snow to melt before moving. It walks through it, low and warm, and the warmth is the only color. The long middle is its own kind of country. You are warmer than the day looks.

**Prompt.** *What does the long middle look like for you right now? Where are you warmer than your surroundings suggest?*

---

### Cycle 7 — Rowing POV (2 year coin · agency, momentum, you're moving)

**Image.** First-person view from a small rowboat, two oars extending outward from the bottom of the frame into a Soft Periwinkle lake. The horizon is low, the oars are the foreground. No rower is visible — only the oars, the water, the line of distant shore. Mood: agency, motion, your hands doing the work.

**Significance.** You don't see the rower in this image. You see the oars. The work is yours — your hands, your grip, your direction. The view changes because you do.

**Prompt.** *What are you holding the oars of right now? Where does the view change when you adjust your grip?*

---

### Cycle 8 — Resting Horse (3 year coin · rest is part of the work, deep middle)

**Image.** A horse lying in a Soft Mint meadow, head up but body down, legs tucked. Loose line work in Ocean Indigo over a Soft Mint wash. The horse is at rest, but its ears are forward — not asleep, just paused. Sky is Soft Linen negative space. Mood: earned rest, awake pause.

**Significance.** The horse isn't sleeping. It's resting with its ears up — present, but not working. The rest *is* the work. Stopping is not the same as quitting. Pausing is not the same as failing.

**Prompt.** *What is the rest that you've earned but haven't been letting yourself take? Where can you lie down with your ears up?*

---

### Cycle 9 — Desert Landscape (2 month coin · endurance, vastness, the long haul)

**Image.** A wide, horizontal desert at low light. A single set of small tracks leads from the bottom of the frame toward a low horizon. The desert is a Soft Peach wash, the tracks are Ocean Indigo. Sky is Soft Linen, vast. Mood: endurance, the long haul, not lost.

**Significance.** The desert isn't empty — it's full of space. The tracks are the only evidence anyone has walked here. The long haul isn't about scenery; it's about the tracks you're leaving behind.

**Prompt.** *What tracks are you leaving behind you? What evidence is there, in this stretch, that you've walked through?*

---

### Cycle 10 — Frog (3 month coin · leap, transformation, late-middle)

**Image.** A small poison dart frog, vivid and detailed, perched on a dark leaf. The frog is the only saturated color in the frame — rendered in bright Sunrise Coral or Leaf Green. Background is a soft wash of Soft Periwinkle or Soft Sky, suggesting rainforest without filling it. The frog is small in the frame but unmistakably bright. Mood: small but mighty, transformation.

**Significance.** The poison dart frog is small and doesn't try to be big. Its defense isn't size — it's what's already in it. It doesn't grow into something else to be safe. It leans into what's there.

**Prompt.** *What strength do you have that you can lean on — not because you grew into it, but because it was already there?*

---

### Cycle 11 — Koi, 6-Month variant (6 month coin · top seller, persistence, late re-use)

**Image.** A traditional red-orange koi mid-leap, body arched, water cascading off its scales. The koi is frozen mid-jump between two levels of a small waterfall — a fraction of a second. Water is Soft Periwinkle wash; koi is full Sunrise Coral with Ocean Indigo line work. Landscape composition, dynamic. Mood: persistence in motion, the popular motif returning, the leap.

**Significance.** Koi swim upstream because the upstream is where they grow. The 6-month koi isn't fighting the current — it's choosing the current that makes it stronger. The late return of the top motif: the koi is here because you kept choosing it.

**Prompt.** *What's the "upstream" you've been choosing lately? Where has the harder path actually made you stronger?*

> **Note for Barbara.** This is the **6-Month Koi** coin (top seller, ANA catalog #1316799115). Distinct from the 1-yr koi in C3 (above). Suggested differentiation: C11 = landscape, smaller fish, active current, red-orange palette, mid-leap frozen motion. C3 = portrait, larger fish, calm water, gold palette, slow upward angle. Two different koi watercolors, not one reused.

---

### Cycle 12 — Phoenix (1 year coin · rising, renewal, the close)

**Image.** A phoenix rising, wings spread, mid-ascent. Ocean Indigo line work over a Soft Sky and Soft Butter wash suggesting flame and lift. The phoenix is the largest single element in the composition, with negative space below (the ground it left) and color blooming above (the air it's entering). Mood: rising, the close, the turn.

**Significance.** The phoenix doesn't rise because the fire was easy. It rises because the fire happened. Renewal isn't a finish line. It's a turn.

**Prompt.** *What have you risen from? What's the turn you're standing in right now — not an ending, just a turn?*

## 1. Purpose

A journal for **any kind of "recovery journey"** — whether that means:

- Mental health
- Anxiety
- Emotional or physical sobriety
- Addiction recovery
- Grief, divorce, job loss, identity transition
- Or any other path that requires self-examination and reflection

The journal is the tool. The "recovery" is whatever the reader is working through.

---

## 2. Target Customer

- **Adult, male or female.** Not gendered.
- Skew note: ANA's sober coins are mostly purchased by women as gifts for men. A mental-health/recovery journal is likely to skew more female in direct purchase — but the product itself is **not "feminine."** Design and copy must read as gender-neutral / human.
- Self-paced user. Some will fill it daily. Some will open it 1–2x/week. Some will use it sporadically. **The book should work for all of them without shame or wasted pages.**

## 4. Cycle Structure (12×)

A **12-cycle framework** is the spine. Each cycle is one self-contained unit, designed to flex across these use patterns without breaking:

| Use pattern | How the cycles map |
|---|---|
| **Daily journaling** | 1 cycle per week → 12 weeks total |
| **Sporadic 1–2 days/week** | 1 cycle per month → 12 months total |
| **"Whenever I get to it"** | User opens the book on their own schedule; cycles become loose time markers, not a calendar |

The book **does not enforce a pace.** The user writes the date. There is no day counter, no week number, no shame.

---

## 5. Cycle Anatomy

Each of the 12 cycles is built from three components, in this order:

1. **7 reflection pages** — daily-style prompted entries. All 7 pages share the same layout and same anchor art, but **different prompts** per day.
2. **1 two-page recap spread** — full-bleed inspirational art on the left page, short reflection prompts on the right.
3. **1 forward-looking "goals-type" page** — sets an intention or note for the next cycle.

So per cycle: **7 + 2 + 1 = 10 pages** of content (plus the spread counts as 2 physical pages but 1 unit).

## 6. Coin Iconography Tie-Ins

ANA's milestone coins are the visual anchor. They are **nature-based** (torii, desert, frog, stag, kraken, koi, fox, phoenix, waterfall, rowing, horse — see `../PRODUCT_CATALOG_MASTER.md`).

The journal uses the **same iconography, subtly.** This is a tie-in, not a centerpiece:

- 1 motif per cycle, rendered **twice**: once as 0.8" line art on the 7 daily pages, once as full-bleed watercolor on the recap spread (see §7)
- Final page is a coin-grid end cap (see §10)
- The book is *adjacent* to the coins, not a celebration of them

The reader does not need to own a coin to use the journal. But a coin owner will see the visual family and feel the connection.

### 6a. Cycle → Icon Mapping (locked for v3)

The mapping below is the **single source of truth** for which icon motif lives on each cycle's 7 daily pages and recap spread. Carried forward from v2, confirmed by Catherine 2026-06-04.

| Cycle | Icon | Source | Narrative beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Torii | 1 month coin | New journey, threshold, the start |
| 2 | Waterfall & lily | 18 month coin | Serenity, ongoing flow after the start |
| 3 | Two-fish | ANA logo | "Apart not alone" — mutual support, mid-early |
| 4 | Stag | 4 month coin | Quiet strength, grounding, mid-journey |
| 5 | Kraken lighthouse | 5 month coin | Lurking perils + vigilance, mid-journey |
| 6 | Fox in snow | 9 month coin | Solitude, wisdom, the long middle |
| 7 | Rowing POV | 2 year coin | Agency, momentum, you're moving |
| 8 | Resting horse | 3 year coin | Rest is part of the work, deep middle |
| 9 | Desert landscape | 2 month coin | Endurance, vastness, the long haul |
| 10 | Frog | 3 month coin | Leap, transformation, late-middle |
| 11 | Koi (late re-use) | 6 month coin (top seller) | Persistence, popular motif returns late |
| 12 | Phoenix | 1 year coin | Rising again, renewal, the close |

**Narrative arc:** gentle start (water, fish) → growing strength (stag, lighthouse, fox) → action and endurance (rowing, horse, desert, frog) → re-emergence (koi, phoenix). Mirrors a year of recovery/mental-health work without naming it.

**Continuity rule (locked):** The 7 daily pages in a cycle share the same top-of-page line-art anchor. When the user reaches the recap spread at the end of that cycle, the full-bleed watercolor on the left page is the *same* motif, larger. The user feels continuity without being told.

**Asset source:** Catherine has identified a designer in Canva with suitable SVG icons that match the 12 motifs. Confirmed 2026-06-04. SVG format → scales cleanly to both 0.8" line art and full-bleed watercolor source. (Open: which icons match cleanly out-of-the-box vs. need custom re-draw.)

**Koi note (locked):** The 6-month koi (ANA's top seller) is deliberately held until cycle 11 — a late return that gives the top motif weight. The 1-year coin's koi design is *not* used in the journal.

## 8. Daily Page Layout

The 84 daily reflection pages share a common layout. The user sees the same template every day; what changes is the cycle's line-art anchor, the affirmation, the prompts, and the user's own content. Below the page is described top-to-bottom.

### 8a. Header zone

- **Heading:** "Today's Thoughts" — Architect's Daughter, top of page
- **Date line:** directly underneath the heading. User fills in the date themselves
- **Line-art icon:** at top right. 0.8" tall (see §7a). Same motif for all 7 days in a cycle

### 8b. Streak + Affirmation row (two boxes side by side)

- **Left box — "Current streak":** intentionally unfilled at print time. The user defines what they're tracking: sober days, weeks of therapy, days without a panic attack, journal-consistency days, etc. Whatever fits their journey. This makes the journal useful to a wide audience without making it feel like a sobriety tool
- **Right box — daily affirmation:** one affirmation per day, cycles through **84 unique affirmations** (see §8i). Sits in a defined box, not a flowing quote

### 8c. Reflections subheading

- **"Reflections"** — Architect's Daughter, sits between the streak/affirmation row and the prompts. Sets the prompts apart from the boxes above

### 8d. Prompts

- **2 prompts per page**, each with **3 lines to fill in**
- All 7 days within a cycle share the same prompt theme (carried from v2's daily page logic) but the specific question differs per day
- **Variant A/B split is out for v3** (decided 2026-06-04). Every daily page uses the same layout. Differentiation comes from the rotating prompts and affirmations, not from layout variants. Keeps the book visually consistent and easier to author

### 8e. Free space (bottom 1/3 of page)

- Dot grid, bullet-journal style — same convention as v2 (light enough to disappear, gives a writing anchor for users who freeze on a blank page)
- **Cue line at top of free space:** "Free space — use for written thoughts or creative expression"

### 8f. Page numbering

- Format at bottom of page: **`Cycle x-y`** where `x` = cycle number and `y` = page within that cycle
- Example: "Cycle 1-4" = 4th page of Cycle 1
- Small, low contrast, footer-grade

### 8g. Fonts (locked for v3)

- **Headings & subheadings** ("Today's Thoughts", "Reflections"): **Architect's Daughter** — casual, hand-drawn feel
- **Body text & prompts** (the questions, the date line, the cue line): **Avenir**

### 8h. Margins (locked for v3, Lulu-compliant per Catherine 2026-06-04)

| Margin | Size |
|---|---|
| Outside | 0.5" |
| Gutter (inside) | 0.75" |
| Top | 0.75" |
| Bottom | 0.5" |

Open: confirm Lulu coil-binding minimums on the gutter (0.75" should clear; sister is checking).

### 8i. Affirmation content list (open, content item)

**84 unique short daily affirmations** to be authored for v3. One per daily page, cycles through 12 cycles (each cycle sees 7 of the 84). Open items: author (Barbara? Catherine? commissioned?), tone (secular, no Higher Power — carries from v2), length (very short, fits the box), sourcing rules.

Catherine's sister is a former book designer and is being consulted on layout questions (margins, bleed, type fit, coil-binding safety). Her feedback gets folded into §8 as decisions land.

## 9. Tactile Element — Letter to Future Self

A physical, interactive page near the back of the book. The letter-to-self page is the **very last facing (recto, right-hand) page in the book.**

- **Front of the letter page** (the last page the user turns to): instructions to write a letter to their future self, seal it, and **tape the envelope to this page.** Tactile artifact. Optional but encouraged.
- **Back of the letter page** (the verso — the side facing the inside back cover): about ANA and the coins — origin story, link to apartnotalone.com, soft pitch. The reader arrives at this side after they've been working through the book; the ANA intro lands with context, not as an ad.
- **Envelope placement:** the taped envelope sits in the **inside back cover** area. The blank end page (§10) provides the surface for the envelope to lay flat against when the book is closed.

**Open copy items:** letter-page instructions, ANA/coins pitch tone, and the physical envelope size (needs to be specified before printer handoff).

## 10. Page Architecture (v3, locked 2026-06-04)

The page math is locked. **Total: 126 pages.** Math is clean: 2 intro + 120 cycle + 4 end = 126.

| Section | Pages | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Front matter (intro) | 2 | Logo + wordmark on p1; welcome / "why this journal exists" on p2 |
| **Cycle pages (all 12 cycles)** | **120** | **12 × 10 = 120.** See cycle math below |
| **End pages** | **4** | **Coin-grid end cap, Letter-to-self, Resources, Blank** (in this order, before back cover) |
| **Total** | **126** | ✓ Clean math |

**Cycle math (locked 2026-06-04, v3 update from v2):**
- **All 12 cycles = 10 pages each.** 12 × 10 = 120
- Per cycle: 7 dailies + 1 goal page + 2 recap spread = 10 pages
- **v2's "cycle 1 has no goals" and "cycle 12 ends on recap" rules are retired in v3.** Each cycle has the same internal structure. Simpler, more uniform, easier to author

**End page order (locked):**

1. **Coin-grid end cap** — full-color 11-coin grid, framing copy, apartnotalone.com URL. One application of the journal, not the only one
2. **Letter-to-self envelope page** — last facing page in the book. Envelope taped to verso, sits in inside back cover (see §9)
3. **Resources page** — 1 page (per Catherine 2026-06-04: 1 page is enough; the v2 2-page spread was overcomplicated). Crisis lines, professional help, ANA links, sober + mental health framing
4. **Blank page** — structural / placeholder. Purpose TBD (most likely the surface the envelope lays against when the book is closed, or inside front cover)

**Spec:** 6×9 coil-bound, 60lb uncoated interior, matte cover. **Print partner:** Lulu (Morrisville, NC). **Even page count** required for Lulu coil binding (126 is even ✓).

**v2 carryover:** Spec, print partner, page architecture concept, and most layout details carry forward. v3 updates: math is now clean (126 ✓), end page order locked, cycle math simplified to uniform 10 pages/cycle, daily page layout spec'd in detail (§8), fonts and margins locked.

## 11. End Goal — CSV Build

The deliverable is a **CSV that drives production.** Each row = one journal page (126 rows total).

**Schema drafted — see [`journal-csv-schema-v3.md`](./journal-csv-schema-v3.md).** 19 columns covering: page number, cycle, page-in-cycle, page type, section, line-art ID, art ID (recap), tint ID, heading, subheading, body, affirmation, prompts 1–3, cue, page label, status, and notes. Renderer constants (e.g., "Today's Thoughts" heading, "Reflections" sub-heading, streak box label) are baked into the renderer and not stored per-row, keeping the CSV focused on what actually varies.

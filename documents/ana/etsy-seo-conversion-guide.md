## Key Findings from Competitor Analysis

- **Simpler titles** — "One Year Sobriety coin" vs keyword-stuffed

- **Social proof** — 5.0 stars, 10,000+ sales
- **Higher price** — $24.99 (works as quality signal)

## Traffic Drivers

### 1. Title Optimization
- **What works:** Simple, clear titles with primary keyword first
- **TheAchievemint example:** "One Year Sobriety coin"
- **ANA (pre-March):** Short titles
- **Current issue:** Etsy AI may have keyword-stuffed beyond readability

**Recommendation:** Test shorter titles on:
- 1 Year Koi (top performer)
- 6 Month Koi (volume driver)
- 3 Year (highest conversion)

### 2. Tags (Etsy-specific SEO)
- 13 tags per listing = free real estate
- Should include: synonyms, alternative searches, misspellings
- Refresh quarterly

### 3. Photography
- Etsy prioritizes "lifestyle" shots in algorithm
- First image = most important (currently clean studio)
- **Idea:** Use AI to generate coin-in-hand mockups
  - Canva AI can generate images
  - Runway/Pika/LipSync for video
  - Could create "lifestyle" first image without new photography

### 4. Video
- Listings with video = higher in search
- Etsy includes 1 video per listing (free)
- Catherine's idea: Use AI to create small "coin in hand" video
- Canva has AI video generation
- Alternative: Runway, Pika Labs, or even LipSync

### 5. Freshness
- Etsy rewards active shops
- Update listings periodically (even small changes)
- "Renew" listings occasionally (costs $0.20)

## Conversion Drivers

### 1. Description Strategy
- **Current ANA:** Scannable, mobile-first, bullet points
- **TheAchievemint:** Story-driven, emotional, flowing prose

**Insight:** Mobile scanability may have sacrificed emotional resonance.

**Test:** Try richer descriptions on 2-3 top listings:
- 1 Year Koi
- 3 Year (highest converter)

### 2. Price Testing
- Current: ~$19 + $5.48 shipping = ~$24.50
- TheAchievemint: $24.99 + $5.48 = $30.47

**Theory:** Lower price might signal lower quality to browsers.
**Test:** Try raising price by $2-3 on premium items (1 Year, 3 Year)

### 3. Social Proof
- Need more reviews — 5.0 stars would help enormously
- Consider: Post-purchase follow-up to encourage reviews

### 4. Cross-sell / Bundles
- "Buy together, get free shipping" drives AOV
- Could bundle: 6 Month + 1 Year (first year set)
- Or: Add related POD product (journals, keychains)

### 5. Shop Activity
- Etsy algorithm favors active shops
- Keep adding related products (POD, accessories)
- Update about section periodically

## Detailed Findings

### DoingItSober (doingitsober.com)
**Categories beyond medallions:**
- **Apparel**: T-shirts, tees, tanks for recovery community
- **Jewelry**: Pendants, necklaces, bracelets (sterling silver)
  - AA 1" Shiny Gold Pendant w/Necklace: $24.99
  - Love Peace and Recovery Sterling Silver bracelet: $153.97
  - Love Peace and Recovery Sterling Silver Necklace: $179.00
  - Celtic Triskele Silver Pendant: $99.97
- **Medallion Holders**: Wooden plaque holders ("Just For Today" $18.99)
- **Ornaments**: AA Metal Christmas Tree Recovery Ornament ($11.00)
- **Recovery Gifts**: Various themed gifts

**Key insight:** Broadest product range beyond coins. Full lifestyle brand.

---

### TheAchieveMint (theachievemint.com)
**Categories beyond coins:**
- **Race Medals**: Event service for race directors
- **On-site Personalization**: Engraving (name, time, date) while-you-wait
- **Medals**: Engraved per hour service

**Key insight:** B2B event focus. Coins/medals are separate consumer shop.

---

### LibertyLaserUSA (libertylaserusa.com)
**Categories beyond coins:**
- **Drinkware**: Laser-engraved drinkware
- **Personalized Keepsakes**: Custom laser-engraved products
- **Small-batch Craftsmanship**: Fast turnaround

**Key insight:** General laser engraving - recovery is one vertical, not their sole focus.

---

### Unity16 (unity16.com)
**Categories beyond tokens:**
- **Tri-plate Tokens**: AA medallions
- **Bumper Stickers**: Recovery-themed stickers
- **Virtual Tokens**: Digital/phone-based tokens for online sharing

**Key insight:** Focus on AA meeting offices, Alano clubs. B2B wholesale model. Recently took Etsy store down to focus on local offices.

---

### SaltyPotatoChips (saltypotatochips.com)
**Categories:**
- **"Chips"**: Celebratory medallions for all occasions
- **Non-program specific**: Recovery-themed but not AA/NA specific

**Key insight:** Brand is playful ("Fifth Pocket"). Focused on celebratory medallions, not just recovery.

---

### SoberSerenityShop (Etsy only)
**Etsy stats:** ~1 year on platform, 349 sales
**Non-coin products:**
- **Sticker packs**: sobriety + other themes
- **Pins**: Best-sellers based on reviews
- **Medallions**: Themed medallions (not milestone-based, except 1 year)
- **"Engraved" crystals**: Decorative items
- **Bracelets**: Low-cost imports from China

**Key insight:** Low coin volume (<25% of sales). Stickers and pins are primary reviews. Stocked inventory (not POD). Good add-on model - affordable items that pair with coins.

---

### TheLonelyMoose (Etsy only)
**Non-coin products:**
- **Keychains**: Hand-stamped metal keychains ($40-$55)
- **Personalized Jewelry**: Hand-stamped metal items
- **Gift Items**: Broader than just coins

**Key insight:** Highest volume on Etsy. Sells much more than coins - full lifestyle brand.

---

### idealgiftings (Etsy only)
**Non-coin products:**
- **Challenge Coins**: Broader than recovery
- **General Gift Items**: Broader market appeal

**Key insight:** Recovery + broader challenge coins market.

## Opportunities for ANA

### Gaps in competitor offerings:
1. **Secular-only positioning** - Most competitors lean spiritual/AA-heavy
2. **Mugs/drinkware** - Only LibertyLaserUSA and DoingItSober do this
3. **Stickers** - Only Unity16 (bumper stickers)
4. **Jewelry** - Only DoingItSober has significant jewelry
5. **Apparel** - Only DoingItSober
6. **Tactile/fidget items** - None found (opportunity!)
7. **Gift sets/bundles** - Limited cross-sell presence

### Quick wins to consider:
- Medallion holders (DoingItSober proves demand at $18.99)
- Recovery-themed drinkware
- Secular-focused jewelry (avoid "God/HP" language)
- Bundle sets (coin + something extra)

## Notes
- ANA's edge: Truly secular (no "Higher Power" language), clean design
- Most competitors either: (a) spiritual/AA-focused or (b) generic challenge coins
- Room for "lifestyle brand" expansion once coin foundation is solid

## Tone Guide

**What works:**
- Self-aware irony (laughing with, not at)
- Survival humor — "I made it" energy
- Gift-buying friendly (someone buying for someone else can laugh too)
- Minimal, not heavy — not "struggle" vibes, more "I made it through"

**What to avoid:**
- Preachy ("Choose happy!")
- Sad ("My journey")
- Religious/spiritual language (ANA's secular edge)
- Overly earnest — save that for the competition

---

## Vibe Check

- Target: Women 25-45, gift-buyers
- Voice: Millennial, internet-native, self-aware
- Humor: Dry, not desperate
- Design: Clean, minimal, some dark humor allowed

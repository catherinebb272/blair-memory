## Design Constraints (What Can't Change)

- **Months on front:** Intentional design choice — coins say "xx months" on BACK only to avoid outing someone with a keyring. This limits ability to compete on "at a glance" clarity.
- **Extended milestones:** Barbara/Drew haven't created 15-month, 20-month, etc. Extended range not coming soon.
- **Photography:** No bandwidth to retake all images. Current images are mixed (some head-on, some angled).

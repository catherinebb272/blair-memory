**SEO note:** Etsy's announcement field isn't indexed for search the way titles/tags are, but the words *are* visible to buyers. "Outer Banks," "watercolor," "digital download" all reinforce brand keywords without stuffing.

## 4. Recommended Shop Icon

*Etsy requires a square icon, 500×500 px minimum, displayed at small sizes (32–50 px) in search results, on listings, on the shop homepage, and on the owner profile.*

**Recommendation: the compact "Saltwash" wordmark as the icon.**

**Recipe (in Canva, 500×500 canvas):**

1. **Background:** `#E8DCC4` (Sand) — full canvas, square.
2. **Type:** "Saltwash" — Playfair Display, Regular or Medium weight. **Not Bold.**
3. **Size:** ~180pt (fills ~65% of canvas width).
4. **Color:** `#2C3E4A` (Storm Navy).
5. **Position:** centered, both axes.
6. **Padding:** ~100 px on all sides.
7. **No** drop shadow, outline, background shape, rotation, or third element.

**Why the wordmark, not an illustration:**
- The wordmark is Saltwash's biggest brand asset. Putting it on every listing thumbnail, search result, and shop page means buyers see the brand name *dozens of times* before they click.
- Etsy's icon shows up at 32–50 px in search results. Playfair (high-contrast serif) survives that downscale cleanly. A small illustration tends to get muddy.
- It mirrors the brand kit Section 17 decision: full lockup (`Saltwash Prints`) for the banner, compact wordmark (`Saltwash`) for avatar/icon.

**Backup option: a monogram** (if the wordmark feels too long at 32 px)
- `SP` set in Playfair Display, capitals only, regular weight, Storm Navy on Sand. Centered, ~280 pt.
- Less distinctive than the wordmark, but legible at the smallest sizes.

**Don't:**
- Don't put the wordmark on white. Sand is the brand's ground; the icon will sit next to a lot of white in Etsy's UI.
- Don't add a wave, sun, or dune graphic behind the text.
- Don't use a tagline in the icon. No room, and the taglines are too long.
- Don't go all-caps here. Breaks the sentence-case rule and mismatches the banner lockup.

**File export:** PNG, sRGB, 500×500 px.

## 5. Order Receipt Banner

*Etsy dimensions: 760 × 100 px. Appears on every buyer's order receipt (PDF + email). Smaller horizontal space than the shop banner, so the design is tighter. Etsy's auto-message policy does not restrict banner content — this is a graphic, not a message.*

**Recommendation: Wordmark + tagline, centered, with a thin horizon-line accent.**

**Recipe (in Canva, 760×100 custom canvas):**

1. **Background:** `#E8DCC4` (Sand) — full canvas.
2. **Top line:** a 1 px horizontal line in `#93A3AC` (Mist), ~520 px wide, centered horizontally, positioned ~10 px from the top edge. *(This is the "horizon" — a brand signature.)*
3. **Wordmark:** "Saltwash Prints" — Playfair Display, Regular, ~32–36 pt, `#2C3E4A` (Storm Navy), centered, positioned just below the horizon line.
4. **Tagline:** "Quiet scenes from the edge of the sea." — Lato Light Italic (or Regular Italic), ~11–12 pt, `#6B5D4F` (Driftwood), centered, with ~4–6 pt space below the wordmark.
5. **Bottom line:** a 1 px horizontal line in `#93A3AC` (Mist), same width and position as the top line, ~10 px from the bottom edge.
6. **Vertical padding:** ~10–12 px top, ~10–12 px bottom, lines on both sides as visual bookends.

**Why this layout:**
- The two thin lines turn a flat 760×100 strip into something that reads as *framed* — a quiet echo of the way the prints themselves are framed.
- Wordmark + tagline is more brand-reinforcing than wordmark alone, and the order receipt is one of the highest-attention moments for a buyer (they're looking at it). Reinforcing the tagline here is high-value real estate.
- The horizon line is on-brand. Saltwash's art is mostly horizon-and-water compositions; the receipt banner echoing that motif is a small but real signature.
- Sand + Storm Navy + Driftwood + Mist — all four brand colors are in the banner, no element feels out of place.

**Alternatives if the horizon line feels like too much:**

- **A: Wordmark only** — "Saltwash Prints" centered, Storm Navy on Sand, no lines. Cleanest possible. Use this if the framed version reads too busy at 100 px height.
- **B: Wordmark + tagline, no lines** — middle ground. Two text elements, no extra graphics.
- **C: Wordmark left, tagline right** — left-aligned wordmark, right-aligned tagline. More like a letterhead. Good if Catherine wants a "correspondence" feel.

**Don't:**
- Don't use the "Painted from the dunes." tagline here — that's for print cards and product tags, not the brand's primary surface.
- Don't include a thank-you message. The receipt already has one (Etsy auto-generates "Thank you for your order"). Adding another is redundant.
- Don't add the shop icon or a graphic mark. At 100 px tall, anything more than type + a single line accent will look crowded.
- Don't go all-caps. Same reasoning as the shop icon — breaks the sentence-case rule.

**File export:** PNG, sRGB, 760×100 px. Etsy's uploader accepts up to 2 MB.

## 7. About the Shop

> Saltwash Prints is a family-run art studio, two generations working from Texas with a long-running love affair with the North Carolina coast.
>
> We start with photography — mostly from the Outer Banks, that long, windswept stretch of barrier islands where the houses sit alone on the dunes and the light does something it doesn't do anywhere else. Each print begins as one of those photographs. The image is then translated into watercolor in our studio: the painted version is made in the same spirit as the photo, with the same eye for the part of the scene most people walk past. Quiet, place-specific, honest about what's not in the frame. No crowds, no parking lots, no perfect sunsets. Just dunes, water, weather, and the kind of stillness you can't stage.
>
> Beyond the coastal prints, we keep our hands in a number of other things — paper goods, small mixed media, whatever catches the studio's eye that season. We like the work to be small-batch and personal, made the way the photograph is taken: deliberately, slowly, with an eye for the parts most people walk past.

>
> Saltwash Prints is based in Texas. The salt air is in our heads.

**Why this version works:**

- **"Two generations" + "family-run"** — vague enough to be true (Catherine is the founder, the "two generations" framing is the part Catherine said to keep soft), specific enough to be warm. Doesn't name anyone.
- **"Working from Texas with a long-running love affair with the North Carolina coast"** — names the location constraint and the location inspiration in the same breath. Doesn't apologize for the Texas address; reframes it as a connection point.
- **"The image is then translated into watercolor in our studio"** — passive voice does the honest-but-vague work. Doesn't claim Catherine paints, doesn't claim the studio paints, just says the watercolor version is made. - **"Made in the way the photograph is taken"** — the "two generations" loop: Catherine's way of working is what she was taught, the way she teaches.
- **"The salt air is in our heads"** — closes the About the way the announcement closes. Same line, same breath. Buyers who see both pages will feel the signature.

## 8. Shop Policies (digital-only v1)

*Etsy's "Shop Policies" page is a single page with sections. Most fields accept plain text. Use Etsy's default for anything not covered here.*

### Payment Policy

> All payments are processed securely through Etsy's checkout. Saltwash Prints doesn't see or store your payment information — that's handled entirely by Etsy.
>
> Accepted payment methods include credit card, debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and any other methods Etsy enables in your region.

*Why this version:* buyers on Etsy know the platform. This just confirms the standard. Don't try to explain Etsy's payment system — they're already on it.

### Shipping Policy

> Saltwash Prints sells digital downloads only — no physical items will be shipped.
>
> After your purchase, your file is available in two places: in your Etsy account under Purchases, and in the receipt email Etsy sends you. If the email lands in spam, the link in your Etsy account still works.
>
> If neither location shows your file, message us through Etsy and we'll resend.

*Why this version:* consistent with the digital download message. Reinforces the "where do I find my file" answer in a place buyers will look for it.

### Returns & Exchange Policy

> All Saltwash prints are digital files delivered instantly after purchase. Because the file is delivered at the moment of sale, all sales are final and we can't accept returns on a file that's been downloaded.
>
> If something is off with the file you receive — it won't open, it's the wrong image, the resolution doesn't match the listing — message us within 7 days. We'll fix it or refund you, whichever you'd prefer.

*Why this version:* Etsy's 2024+ seller policy requires a returns statement. "All sales are final" is the standard for digital. The 7-day window + fix-or-refund carve-out covers the realistic edge cases (corrupted file, wrong file, resolution mismatch) without inviting frivolous refund requests. Being clear here builds trust.

### Cancellations

> Once your order is placed, the file is generated and made available for download. Cancellations aren't possible after that point.
>
> If you change your mind within an hour of purchase and haven't downloaded yet, message us — we can usually catch it and cancel.

*Why this version:* practical courtesy. Most buyers won't cancel, but the line is worth having for the ones who do.

### License (Personal Use)

> Your files are licensed for personal use. Print as many copies as you'd like, frame them, give them as gifts.
>
> Please don't resell or redistribute the digital file itself.

*Why this version:* reinforces the same license from the digital download message. Some buyers only read the policies page, not the listing description, so the license lives in both places.

### Privacy Policy

> Use Etsy's default privacy policy. Saltwash Prints doesn't collect any information beyond what Etsy automatically collects through the checkout process. We don't send marketing emails. We don't share your information with third parties.
>
> (Etsy provides a default privacy policy in the shop setup flow. Use it. The default covers the legal requirements for an Etsy seller, and most buyers never read it. If you ever want a custom one, it can be drafted in a follow-up — but it's not the place to spend time on v1.)

*Why this version:* Etsy's default privacy policy is legally adequate. Don't draft a custom one for v1.

### Contact

> Questions about your order? Message us through Etsy. We usually respond within 24 hours, often faster.

*Why this version:* points to Etsy's messaging system (the right channel), sets a realistic response expectation, no fluff.

### v2 changes when POD/print is added (Phase 2)

When Saltwash adds physical prints via Printful, Gelato, or a local NC printer, the policies page needs a rewrite. Save this list for when the partner is chosen — easier to do the rewrite when the actual production times and ship methods are known.

1. **Shipping Policy** — full rewrite. Production time (Printful 2-7 business days, Gelato 1-5 days, local NC 5-10 days), ship methods and costs, tracking, international shipping, "items ship from [location]."
2. **Returns & Exchange Policy** — major rewrite. Etsy's 2024+ policy requires accepting returns for damaged/wrong items. "If your print arrives damaged, message us within X days with a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund." Buyer-paid return shipping for buyer's-remorse returns. "We don't accept returns on opened/framed items" is the standard POD carve-out.
3. **Cancellations** — add cancellation window before production starts. "Orders can be cancelled within X hours of purchase, before production begins. After production starts, no cancellations."
4. **Add a FAQ section** — "My print arrived damaged" / "When will my order ship" / "Do you ship internationally" / "Can I change the size after ordering."
5. **Keep the License section** for any digital items still in the shop.

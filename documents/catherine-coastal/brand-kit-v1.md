## 1. Brand Positioning

**The one-line version:**
Saltwash Prints makes watercolor prints for people who want their walls to feel like an empty beach in October — soft, windswept, unhurried.

**The longer version:**
We're not the "Beach Vibes" shop. We're not "Coastal Grandma" merch. We're the shop for the buyer who wants their home to feel like a place they've actually been, not a Pinterest board. Each piece starts as a photograph and ends as a painted wash. No crowds, no parking lots, no perfect sunsets. Just dunes, water, and the kind of stillness you can't stage.

**What we are:**
- Quiet, place-specific, painterly
- Outer Banks / mid-Atlantic coast anchor
- Digital downloads primary, POD secondary (or not at all)
- Beige + blue-grey palette, but the palette is the hook, not the brand

**What we are not:**
- Tropical / "Life's a beach" twee
- Bright sunsets, palm trees, generic beach
- Photorealistic — the artistic watercolor is the point
- Wellness-speak / "live laugh coast" energy

## 2. Voice & Tone

**Three rules:**
1. **Slightly literary, never precious.** Sentences can be beautiful, but they should sound like a person who reads, not a copywriting textbook.
2. **Specific over general.** "Outer Banks" beats "coastal." "Dune house" beats "beach house." Place is the moat.
3. **Honest about what it isn't.** "No crowds, no parking lots" is a feature. The negatives describe the aesthetic.

**Sample voice in action:**
- ✅ "Two houses sit on the dunes, alone with the wind."
- ✅ "The kind of stillness you can't stage."
- ❌ "Bring the beach home with this stunning watercolor print!"
- ❌ "Add coastal charm to your space today."

**Punctuation / mechanics:** Use em dashes — like this — not hyphens. Sentence fragments are fine when they're doing work. Don't end sentences with exclamation points.

## 6. Photography / Art Style Guide

**Subject matter (what to make more of):**
- Single human figures or none — never crowds
- Architecture in landscape (dune houses, lighthouses, piers)
- Animals in scale (one dog, one bird, one horse) dwarfed by environment
- Empty horizons, open water, weather
- Hands, feet, edges of objects (the person implied, not seen)

**Subject matter (what to avoid):**
- Crowds, families, kids, couples
- Bright umbrellas, beach chairs, towels — kitsch
- Palm trees, tropical fish, flamingos
- Photorealism that looks like Midjourney defaults

**Rendering style:**
- Watercolor wash with line work is the primary
- Painterly / loose over tight / realistic
- Edges can be soft, even unfinished-looking
- Negative space is a feature, not a problem to fill

**Composition principles:**
- Off-center subject (rule of thirds, not dead center)
- Lots of sky / lots of water — the figure is small
- Horizon lines, not vertical action
- Quiet, not dramatic

**Mobile-safe check:** When Etsy crops to portrait on mobile, the wordmark in the left third may get cut. If this matters, push the wordmark slightly more toward center, or use a single stacked version: wordmark on top, tagline below, image filling the right.

## 14. Sizes, Pricing & Competitive Landscape

### Competitive landscape (verified 2026-06-16)

Catherine searched Etsy using "coastal" and related phrases. **Found nothing in the Saltwash lane** — nothing place-specific, nothing painterly-and-quiet, nothing anchored to actual mid-Atlantic coast. The category is saturated with:
- Tropical "Beach Vibes" / palm tree kitsch
- Bright photorealistic sunsets
- "Coastal Grandmother" merch
- Generic watercolor that's pretty but placeless

**Implication:** Saltwash's positioning is unoccupied. The "specific Outer Banks anchor + painterly wash + honest negatives ('no crowds, no parking lots')" combination is a real moat. The brand voice has to keep delivering on the specificity promise — the moment we drift into generic coastal, we lose it.

### Print sizes to offer per listing (recommended)

Don't ship all of A1–A5. Ship **5 files per print** designed in 2:3 ratio (covers the most common US frame sizes including the must-have 16×24):

| File | Pixel size @ 300 DPI | Frame size | Aspect ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5×7 | 1500 × 2100 | 5×7 frame | 2:3 (with margins) |
| 8×10 | 2400 × 3000 | 8×10 frame | 4:5 (slight crop) |
| 11×14 | 3300 × 4200 | 11×14 frame | 4:5 (slight crop) |
| 16×24 | 4800 × 7200 | 16×24 frame ✅ | 2:3 |
| 18×24 | 5400 × 7200 | 18×24 frame | 3:4 |

**Why this set:**
- 16×24 is covered (the must-have).
- 8×10, 11×14, 16×20 are covered (the most-bought home frames).
- 5×7 is the entry-tier / small-space option.
- 18×24 is the statement size.
- 2:3 ratio dominates, so 4:5 files are a soft crop — buyers won't notice at frame size.

**Skip for now:** A4/A3 Euro sizes, 24×36, square formats. Add later if Euro buyers ask or sales data shows demand.

### Pricing tiers (recommended)

| Product | Price | Discount vs. singles | Margin note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single print (5 files) | **$9.99** | — | Etsy sweet spot for digital watercolor |
| Bundle of 3 | **$24.99** | ~17% off | Best-increase-AOV |
| Bundle of 5 | **$39.99** | ~20% off | Anchor for "buy the set" |
| Bundle of 10 (full set) | **$69.99** | ~30% off | Future — once 10 prints live |

**Why this tier:**
- $9.99 is below premium but above commodity. Sits next to the better Etsy watercolor sellers without being a loss-leader.
- Bundle math rewards the "buy the set" behavior — important because wall art buyers often want 2-3 coordinating pieces.
- Leaves room to move up to $12.99 single later if reviews support it (no need to anchor low on day 1).

**Bundle composition:** Mix-and-match any 3 / 5 / 10 prints from the catalog. Don't pre-bundle "Coastal Set A" — the buyer's eye should drive the bundle, not ours.

## 15. Roadmap: Greeting Cards (Phase 2)

**Verdict: yes, sensible extension — but Phase 2, not day 1.**

**Why cards make sense:**
- Same brand voice, same palette, same art → low creative lift
- Cards are a *repeat-purchase* product for print customers (gift add-on, hostess gift, etc.)
- Catherine already has card-sentiment practice via the ANA card series (currently parked)
- Etsy has a huge greeting card market with strong impulse-buy behavior
- Cards open a *physical product* lane, useful for brand legitimacy

**Why not day 1:**
- Cards need a different ops model — POD or print run + ship. Don't add shipping/logistics to a digital-only launch.
- Card sentiments need their own voice rules (the "slightly literary, never precious" rule translates, but shorter and harder)
- Want to learn what compositions resonate in the print line first, so card designs are informed by what already sells

**Phase 2 trigger:** ~3–6 months post-launch, OR after 5+ prints are selling with reviews. Pick whichever comes first.

## 17. Wordmark Setup in Canva (concrete recipe)

**Canvas:** new design, custom size **2000×600** (good for export at multiple sizes).

**Step-by-step:**
1. **Background:** set canvas to `#E8DCC4` (Sand)
2. **Add text box.** Type `Saltwash Prints` exactly
3. **Font:** Playfair Display
4. **Weight:** Regular or Medium. **Skip Bold.**
5. **Size:** ~140pt (adjust so wordmark fills ~60% of canvas width)
6. **Color:** `#2C3E4A` (Storm Navy)
7. **Alignment:** left or center
8. **Letter spacing:** default (do not widen)
9. **No drop shadow, no outline, no background shape, no rotation**

**Two variants to make in the same Canva file:**
- **Full lockup:** `Saltwash Prints` (banner, About, packaging)
- **Compact:** `Saltwash` (social avatar, favicon, watermark)

**Tagline placement on banner:** wordmark in Playfair Storm Navy, then `Quiet scenes from the edge of the sea.` underneath in Lato (italic optional), color Driftwood `#6B5D4F`. Stack them, both left-aligned, breathing room between.

**Common Canva traps to avoid:**
- Don't add a graphic behind the text (wave, sun, circle) — fights the quiet voice.
- Don't mix in a third font.
- Don't rotate the text on an angle.
- Don't go bold.
- Don't put the wordmark on pure white — Sand or Sea Fog background only.

## 18. Image Prep Workflow (upscaling + post-process)

**Why this matters:** wrong upscaler smooths out watercolor texture and the work loses its painterly quality. Use the "Art / Painting" preset, not photo mode.

**Tools:**

| Tool | Cost | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Topaz Gigapixel AI** | ~$100 one-time | Gold standard for watercolor | "Art / Painting" preset only. Photo mode over-smooths. |
| **Upscayl** | Free, open source | Very good quality | "Artwork" model. Runs locally, no cloud, no limits. **Start here** to test quality before paying. |

**Target dimensions (5-file set per print):**

| File | Long edge @ 300 DPI |
|---|---|
| 5×7 | 2100 px |
| 8×10 | 3000 px |
| 11×14 | 4200 px |

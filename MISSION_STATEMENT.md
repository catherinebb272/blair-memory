# Heyron Tutorial Project Mission Statement

**Vision:** To create a concise, user-friendly onboarding experience for the Heyron tutorial project, focusing on speed and value for the "impatient user."

**Goal:** To guide new users through essential setup steps (like connecting Discord and GitHub) and fundamental interactions, enabling them to quickly leverage Heyron's capabilities without being bogged down by technical jargon or lengthy explanations.

**Scope:** Develop a series of HTML modules that are clear, actionable, and progressively build user confidence. Prioritize essential setup and core functionalities, deferring complex or less common features to advanced guides or direct user inquiry.

**Principles:**
- **Brevity & Clarity:** Get to the point quickly. Use simple language.
- **Action-Oriented:** Focus on what the user needs to *do*.
- **Value-Driven:** Highlight the immediate benefits of each step.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Introduce concepts and features gradually.
- **User-Centric:** Design for an "impatient user" who wants results fast.

**Key Outputs:**
- A set of HTML modules for the Heyron tutorial.
- A clear pathway for users to connect essential services (Discord, GitHub).
- A foundational understanding of Heyron's capabilities.

**Target Audience:** New Heyron users, particularly those who prefer direct action and quick results over extensive documentation. 
- Made specifically for less experienced users coming to the platform from the Koerner Office podcast who are looking for new side hustles.

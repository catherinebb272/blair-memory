# Etsy Review Response Templates

**Purpose:** Standardized but personal-sounding responses to Etsy reviews
**Usage:** Select template based on review type, customize with customer's name and specific details

---

## Template 1: The Standard 5-Star

**Best for:** Generic positive reviews, no specific details

> Hi [Name]! So glad [recipient name/the coin] arrived safely and that [it resonated/you found it meaningful]. Thank you for supporting Apart Not Alone — it truly means everything.

---

## Template 2: The Detail Callback

**Best for:** Reviews that mention something specific (packaging, design, handwritten note, etc.)

> [Name]! Love that you mentioned [specific detail they noted — the packaging, the weight, the design, the handwritten letter]. That [element] matters so much to us. Thanks for taking the time to share — hope [recipient] treasures it.

---

## Template 3: The "Oops But You Handled It Gracefully"

**Best for:** The 4-star "wrong coin but it's fine" scenario, or any minor issue they handled well

> [Name] — thank you for being so understanding about the mix-up! Your attitude about turning it into ["a fun gift"/"motivation"/their phrasing] is exactly the spirit of what we do. Sending the correct [month] coin your way now. Grateful for customers like you.

---

## Template 4: The Recipient Reaction

**Best for:** Reviews that mention how the recipient cried, loved it, was moved, etc.

> This made our day: "[quote their line about the recipient's reaction]." That's exactly why we do this. Thank you for sharing that moment with us, [Name].

---

## Template 5: The Repeat/Advocate

**Best for:** Return customers, people buying multiple coins for same recipient

> [Name]! [So great to hear from you again / honored you chose us again for this milestone]. [Recipient name] has quite the collection growing! Thanks for being part of the Apart Not Alone family.

---

## Pro Tips for Drew

1. **Always use their first name** if visible
2. **Copy/paste one phrase they used** (makes it feel personal without thinking)
3. **Keep it under 3 sentences**
4. **Emoji is optional** but one max
5. **Sign as "— Drew"** (not Apart Not Alone, keep it personal)

---

## Template Selection Guide

| Review Type | Template |
|-------------|----------|
| Generic 5-star, no details | Template 1 |
| Mentions specific element (packaging, note, design) | Template 2 |
| Minor issue but handled gracefully | Template 3 |
| Recipient had emotional reaction | Template 4 |
| Repeat customer / multiple purchases | Template 5 |

---

## Example Applications

**Example 1 — Jenn (mentioned handwritten letter, "better than the coin")**
→ Template 2: The Detail Callback

> Jenn! Love that you mentioned the handwritten letter — those notes are my favorite part to write. "Totally unnecessary and yet perfect" is exactly the vibe we were going for. Thank you for such a thoughtful review.

**Example 2 — Elise (repeat buyer, secular option, advocates)**
→ Template 5: The Repeat/Advocate

> Elise! So great to hear from you again — honored you've trusted us with every milestone. "Celebrate people's ongoing hard work and daily choices" — you said it perfectly. See you at year four!

**Example 3 — graysen (short, mentions husband loves them)**
→ Template 4: The Recipient Reaction

> This made our day: "he loves them all." That's exactly why we do this — for the person on the other side. Thanks for sharing that, graysen, and for being a loyal customer.

**Example 4 — Family collection, mentions notes**
→ Template 2 or 5

> Thank you for trusting us with your family's milestones! "Going above and beyond to make sure people feel seen" — that's the goal. Grateful to be part of your journey.

# Competitive Research Summary

## 1. Studio Tuesday

- **Owner / Artist:** Traditional watercolor artist, also works in graphic styles.
- **Location:** Maryland, USA.
- **Etsy Tenure:** 17 years on Etsy.
- **Sales:** ~41 k total sales.
- **Core Product Focus:** Coastal‑inspired watercolors (prints, stickers, journals).
- **Best‑selling Item:** Watercolor plover print (5×7, $19, free shipping, optional mat $5). Frequently appears in multiple categories (Coastal, Beach, Nautical).
- **Sticker Strategy:** Uses original print artwork to create sticker sheets. Standard 7‑sheet packs priced at $4 (≈3×4 in each). Also offers a larger journal‑sticker pack for $8.
- **Print Production:** Handles printing and mailing in‑house; paper type varies by design. Prints are praised for “looks like a real watercolor” and “frame‑ready”.
- **Customer Feedback:** 
  - “Cards were so stunning I framed them.”
  - “I ended up buying 3 prints – very cost effective.”
  - “Print matches description – looks like an actual watercolor.”
  - One buyer mentioned purchasing the artwork in a local store, indicating a **retail channel** beyond Etsy.
- **Reviews:** Recent reviews mention ferns and blue‑crab images; plover has sales but few reviews (likely new or low‑review volume).
- **Tag Insight:** Exact tag list not directly visible, but titles and descriptions suggest tags such as `coastal wall art`, `watercolor print`, `beach decor`, `nautical art`, `sea life illustration`, `5x7 art print`, `planner stickers`, `coastal stickers`, etc.
- **Marketing Highlights:** Emphasizes hand‑painted quality, free shipping, optional mat, and use of the same artwork across multiple product types (prints → stickers → journals).

## 2. BellisiCasaGifts

- **Shop Focus:** Sticker‑only store with themed seasonal collections.
- **Age & Sales:** 6 years on Etsy, ~43 k total sales.
- **Core Product Structure:** 
  - Each **theme** (summer, fall, etc.) contains **10‑15 sets**.
  - A set consists of **7 sheets** (mix of smaller and larger items, a “people” page, a “elements” page resembling washi‑tape/strips, and a sheet of complex mini‑print‑style images).
- **Pricing Model:** 
  - Base price: **$3.84 per sheet**.
  - Dropdown options in the description allow customers to purchase **1 sheet, 2 sheets, or the full 7‑sheet set**, with progressive discounts.
- **Mail Club:** 
  - Subscription‑style “postal mail club” priced at **$11.09 (on sale)**.
  - 163 reviews for the mail club alone, indicating strong repeat‑buyer community.
- **Marketing Caveat:** Description can be misleading – it lists a 7‑sheet set but the purchase options are split across multiple price points. Customers appear accustomed to this structure.
- **Product Themes:** Seasonal (summer, fall, etc.) with consistent visual language—colorway‑specific, combining simple icons, decorative strips, and more detailed mini‑prints.
- **Customer Base:** Likely planner enthusiasts and sticker collectors who value **variety** and **seasonal refreshes**.

## 3. Implications for Saltwash Prints

- **Cross‑product Leverage:** Both shops reuse artwork across different formats (prints → stickers). Saltwash can adopt the same strategy: design a watercolor print, then automatically generate matching sticker sheets and journal stickers.
- **Pricing Benchmarks:** 
  - **5×7 watercolor prints** around $19 with free shipping (Studio Tuesday).
  - **Sticker sheets** ~$4 for 7 sheets (Studio Tuesday) and $3.84 per sheet with volume discounts (BellisiCasaGifts). 
  - Consider a **tiered sticker pricing** (1‑sheet, 2‑sheet, full‑set) to mirror BellisiCasaGifts and increase average order value.
- **Seasonal Collections:** BellisiCasaGifts’ seasonal set model suggests Saltwash could launch **season‑specific sticker collections** (e.g., “Summer Beach”, “Autumn Tide”) that align with the print themes.
- **Mail Club / Subscription:** The successful mail club (163 reviews) indicates a **high‑repeat purchase** model. Saltwash could test a low‑cost subscription (e.g., monthly “Sticker of the Month” or “Print & Sticker Combo”) to boost repeat buyers.
- **SEO & Tagging:** Even without exact tag lists, the high‑performing tags for coastal/watercolor prints and stickers are evident. Use similar tag structures and include seasonal/collection keywords.
- **Customer Communication:** Prompt buyers (especially for new print designs) to leave reviews, as early high‑cart items may have few reviews (as seen with the plover). Leverage the “free shipping” badge for algorithmic boost.

## Origin Story
Drew’s recovery experience (liver failure, transplant) inspired the creation of ANA to provide meaningful sobriety‑milestone coins. The story is woven into the Etsy bio and pinned Instagram post.

## Buyer Profile (CORRECTED 2026-10-04)
**Audience is consistent across ALL ANA products: gift-givers, primarily women buying for men.** Not the recipient.
- Coins: gift purchases (reviews confirm), not self-purchases
- Sober birthday cards: bought by friends of someone in sobriety, NOT for the buyer
- Feelings cards: bought by friends for someone struggling, NOT for the buyer
- **Implication:** coins↔cards cross-sell is symmetric (same buyer, different occasion) — NOT a funnel from a different audience
- **Cards' strategic role:** alternate front door for the same gift-giver audience via different (non-sobriety) search terms — "friendship card," "thinking of you," "funny friend"
- **Language for all ANA copy:** gift-giver voice, never self-purchase voice

## Strategic Constraints
- **2FA lockout** on ANA shop – requires authenticator app and separate manager for continuity.
- **Copy style** – avoid triple‑negation lists and repeated triads; keep language concise.
- **Voice guidelines** – maintain direct, witty tone; no filler phrases.
